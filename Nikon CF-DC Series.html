Product Name:

Nikon CF-DC Series (Semi-Soft Cases)

Price:

$279

Description:

Nikon CF-DC series semi-soft cases are specifically designed to protect Nikon DSLR and Mirrorless cameras. These cases are for photographers who want to shield their cameras from dust, dirt, and light scratches when they are not in a bag or are hanging around the neck. Its design is sleek and minimalist, fitting the natural shape of the camera without adding any unnecessary weight.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Available in various models (such as CF-DC4, CF-DC8) that fit Nikon D-series and Z-series cameras
  • Material: Made from high-quality flexible nylon and synthetic fabric that protects against light rain and dust
  • Closure Type: Quick-release velcro or flap system to ensure there is no delay in removing the camera
  • Storage: Designed specifically for a camera body with one attached lens
  • Portability: Extremely lightweight and foldable design so it can be kept in a pocket when not in use

Key Features:

  • Snug Fit Design: This case is customized according to the camera body and lens, preventing the camera from moving inside.
  • Internal Protection: The soft inner lining protects the camera screen and body from scratches.
  • Lightweight & Portable: It is much lighter than bulky bags, making it excellent for short trips.
  • Weather Resistance: The material is dust-resistant and protects the gear from light moisture.
  • Quick Access: Its simple opening system allows you to quickly take out the camera and capture a shot.
