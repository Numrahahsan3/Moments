Product Name:

Nikon CB-N2200

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon CB-N2200 is a dedicated body case and shoulder bag combo designed specifically for Nikon 1 series compact mirrorless cameras, particularly the J4 and S2 models. This bag is ideal for photographers who value both the safety of their gear and a stylish appearance. Its design provides a perfect fit for the camera body, protecting it from dust, dirt, and scratches.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Specifically designed for Nikon 1 J4 and Nikon 1 S2 mirrorless cameras
  • Construction: Two-piece design (Body case and outer wrap) for maximum accessibility and protection
  • Material: High-quality synthetic leather/fabric with a soft interior lining
  • Mounting: Screws directly into the camera's tripod socket for a secure and stable fit
  • Weight: Extremely lightweight (Approx. 120g - 150g depending on configuration)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 110 x 85 x 45 mm (Custom fit for small form factor)

Key Features:

  • Custom Fit Design: This bag fits snugly against the camera body, ensuring that the camera's size does not feel unnecessarily bulky.
  • Quick Access: The cover opens easily so you can take shots quickly without having to fully remove the camera from the bag.
  • Protective Interior: Equipped with a soft inner lining that protects the camera's LCD screen and body from being scratched.
  • Direct Tripod Mounting: Even with the case attached, you can still mount the camera on a tripod, which is very convenient.
  • Elegant Look: The synthetic leather finish provides a premium and professional appearance.
