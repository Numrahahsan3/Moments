Product Name:

N10 Remote Grip

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon MC-N10 is a specialized remote grip designed for videographers and filmmakers who need seamless control over their Nikon Z-series mirrorless cameras. It mimics the ergonomics of the camera's right-hand grip, allowing users to control essential video functions while the camera is mounted on a gimbal, shoulder rig, or tripod. This tool is essential for small crews or solo shooters who need to adjust settings without breaking their workflow or physical contact with their rig.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Nikon Z9, Z8, Z7 II, Z6 II, and Z6 III (Firmware update may be required)
  • Connection: USB-C (for camera communication and power)
  • Mounting: ARRI-compatible rosette mount
  • Ingress Protection: Dust and drip-resistant (equivalent to Z9 body)
  • Power Source: 2 x AA batteries (Alkaline, Lithium, or NiMH)
  • Battery Life: Up to 12 hours of continuous operation
  • Weight: Approximately 205g (excluding batteries)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 6.6 x 11.6 x 7.3 cm

Key Features:

  • Full Camera Control: Provides access to almost all video settings, including Record, ISO, Exposure Compensation, and the AF-ON button.
  • Seamless Integration: Features the same button layout and click-less command dials as Nikon Z cameras for a familiar and silent operating experience.
  • ARRI Rosette Mount: Uses the industry-standard ARRI-compatible rosette, allowing it to be attached to various cages and handles at any angle.
  • Customizable Buttons: Many buttons (like the Fn1 and Fn2) can be assigned to specific functions via the camera's menu to suit your shooting style.
  • Weather-Sealed Build: Built with the same high standards as flagship Nikon bodies, ensuring it can handle professional use in tough environments.
  • No Latency: Since it uses a wired USB-C connection, there is zero lag between pressing a button and the camera’s response.
