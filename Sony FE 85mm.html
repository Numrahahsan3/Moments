Product Name:

Sony FE 85mm f/1.8

Price:

$598

Description:

The Sony FE 85mm f/1.8 is a highly acclaimed prime lens designed for full-frame E-mount mirrorless cameras. Known for its impressive sharpness and lightweight design, it serves as the perfect portrait lens for both enthusiasts and professionals. With its wide f/1.8 maximum aperture, this lens excels in challenging lighting conditions and delivers a stunningly smooth background blur that allows your subjects to stand out with extraordinary clarity. Despite its professional-grade performance, it remains compact and portable, making it a versatile choice for weddings, events, and everyday street photography.

Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Portrait Specialist)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 (Circular 9-blade diaphragm)
  • Motor: Double Linear Motor for fast and quiet AF
  • Construction: 9 Elements in 8 Groups (ED glass included)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.8m (2.63 ft)
  • Filter Size: 67mm

Key Features:

  • Customizable Focus Hold Button: A dedicated button on the lens barrel that can be assigned to various functions like Eye-AF or focus lock.
  • Fast & Quiet Focus: The double linear motor system ensures rapid, precise, and nearly silent focusing, which is ideal for both high-speed photography and video.
  • Dust & Moisture Resistant: Built with a sealed design to provide reliability in outdoor environments and tricky weather conditions.
  • Compact and Lightweight: Weighing only 371g, it offers incredible portability without compromising on optical quality.
  • Exceptional Sharpness: Optimized to deliver corner-to-corner sharpness, even when shooting wide open at f/1.8.
