Product Name:

Sony LCS-BP3 System Backpack

Price:

$158

Description:

The Sony LCS-BP3 is a high-capacity, professional-grade system backpack designed specifically for photographers and cinematographers who carry extensive gear. This rugged and versatile bag is built to accommodate a full DSLR or Alpha mirrorless setup, including multiple lenses, a vertical grip, and even a laptop up to 15.5 inches. Its ergonomic design features heavily padded shoulder straps and a waist belt to distribute weight evenly, making it ideal for long trekking sessions or travel. The semi-hard casing provides extra protection against impacts, while the customizable interior dividers allow you to organize your equipment precisely how you want it. Whether you are shooting in the city or the wilderness, this backpack ensures your gear stays safe, dry, and easily accessible.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Alpha Series Mirrorless, DSLRs, and professional Camcorders
  • Material: High-quality Polyester with semi-hard protective shell
  • Laptop Compartment: Fits up to 15.5-inch laptop or tablet
  • External Attachments: Integrated Tripod Holder and side pockets
  • Rain Cover: Included (Integrated high-density weather shield)
  • Weight: Approximately 1,550g (54.7 oz)
  • Dimensions: 33.0 x 48.0 x 25.0 cm (13.0 x 18.9 x 9.9 in)

Key Features:

  • Spacious & Customizable: Features flexible internal dividers that can be rearranged to fit 1-2 camera bodies, 3-4 lenses, flashes, and accessories.
  • Ergonomic Comfort: Designed with breathable mesh padding on the back and adjustable chest/waist straps for maximum comfort during long use.
  • Quick Access: Strategically placed side and top openings allow you to grab your camera quickly without taking the entire bag off.
  • Maximum Protection: Built with a semi-hard structure to absorb shocks and a dedicated water-resistant rain cover for extreme weather conditions.
  • Travel Ready: Meets standard airline carry-on requirements and includes a dedicated luggage strap for easy attachment to trolley handles.
