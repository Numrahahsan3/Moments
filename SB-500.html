Product Name:

SB-500

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon SB-500 Speedlight is a compact, lightweight, and easy-to-use flash unit ideal for both photographers and videographers. Its standout feature is the built-in high-intensity LED light, which provides constant illumination for video shooting. It supports i-TTL flash control and is part of Nikon’s Creative Lighting System (CLS), allowing it to be used in wireless lighting setups. The head can rotate 180 degrees and tilt up to 90 degrees, making it perfect for bounce lighting.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed for Nikon DSLR and Z-series cameras supporting i-TTL
  • Guide Number: 24 m (at ISO 100)
  • Power Source: Two AA-size batteries (Alkaline or Ni-MH)
  • Wireless Flash Mode: Commander mode (on compatible cameras) and Remote mode
  • Weight: Approximately 226g (without batteries)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 67 x 114.5 x 70.8 mm

Key Features:

  • Integrated LED Light: Features a dedicated 100 lux LED light, ideal for close-up photography and video recording.
  • Simple Operation: The interface is very straightforward with only a few buttons, making it perfect for beginners.
  • Flexible Bounce Head: The flash head tilts up 90° and rotates 180° left/right to bounce light off walls or ceilings.
  • i-TTL Intelligence: Works with Nikon’s intelligent Through-The-Lens (i-TTL) system to automatically calculate the correct light output for every shot.
  • Compact & Lightweight: Small enough to fit easily in a pocket or small bag, making it best for travel photography.
  • Wireless Control: Can be operated as a master or remote flash within the Nikon Creative Lighting System.
