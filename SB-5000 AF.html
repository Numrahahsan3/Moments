Product Name:

Nikon SB-5000 AF Speedlight

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon SB-5000 AF Speedlight is a high-performance flash designed to meet the rigorous demands of professional photographers in the field and the studio. As Nikon's first Speedlight to feature a built-in cooling system, it allows for up to 100 consecutive shots at full output without thermal shutdown. It introduces a refined radio-controlled Advanced Wireless Lighting system, which works seamlessly with the WR-R10 wireless remote to overcome line-of-sight obstacles. Compact yet powerful, it’s the go-to lighting solution for wedding, editorial, and action photographers who require speed and versatility.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Optimized for Nikon FX and DX-format cameras with i-TTL support
  • Guide Number: 34.5 m (at 35mm) or 55 m (at 200mm) at ISO 100
  • Wireless Communication: Radio-controlled Advanced Wireless Lighting and Optical triggering
  • Input Terminals: External power source terminal, Sync terminal, and PC terminal
  • Weight: Approximately 420g (Body only)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 73.0 x 137.0 x 103.5 mm

Key Features:

  • Radio Control AWL: Operates via radio waves, allowing the flash to work through walls or around corners when paired with the WR-R10/WR-R11a/b controllers.
  • World’s First Cooling System: Features an internal heat-reduction system that enables over 100 continuous full-power firings without the flash overheating.
  • Streamlined Design: Smaller and more compact than previous flagship models, with an improved interface and dedicated 'Info' button for quick access.
  • Flexible Tilting Head: The flash head tilts down to -7° or up to 90°, and rotates 180° left and right for versatile bounce lighting.
  • Wide Zoom Range: Covers a broad focal length range from 24mm to 200mm (14mm with the built-in wide panel).
  • Unified Flash Control: Allows settings to be controlled directly from a compatible Nikon D-SLR or Z-series camera menu.
