Product Name:

Sony Alpha a390

Price:

$450

Description:

The Sony Alpha a390 is a user-friendly DSLR designed specifically for beginners and hobbyists who want to step up from point-and-shoot cameras. Featuring a redesigned ergonomic grip, it provides exceptional comfort and stability for all-day shooting. This camera combines a high-resolution CCD sensor with Sony’s innovative Quick AF Live View technology, allowing you to compose shots easily using the tiltable LCD screen without sacrificing autofocus speed. Whether you are capturing family gatherings, scenic landscapes, or weekend adventures, the a390 delivers rich colors and sharp details with minimal effort, making professional-looking photography accessible to everyone.

Specifications:

  • Sensor: 14.2 MP APS-C CCD Sensor
  • Processor: BIONZ Image Processor
  • AF System: 9-Point Center Cross AF
  • ISO Range: 100 - 3200
  • Display: 2.7-inch Tiltable Clear Photo LCD

Key Features:

  • Quick AF Live View: Enables fast and accurate phase-detection autofocus even when using the LCD screen for framing.
  • SteadyShot INSIDE: Built-in image stabilization compensates for camera shake, allowing for sharper handheld photos with any A-mount lens.
  • Intuitive Graphic Manual: An on-screen help guide that explains camera settings in simple terms, perfect for learning photography.
  • Tiltable LCD Screen: Adjust the angle of the screen to capture unique perspectives from high or low angles with ease.
  • HDMI Output: Easily connect the camera to your HDTV to view your photos and slideshows in stunning high definition.
