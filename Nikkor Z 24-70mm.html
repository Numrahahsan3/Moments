Product Name:

Nikkor Z 24-70mm

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikkor Z 24-70mm f/4 S is a versatile zoom lens designed for Nikon's Z-mount mirrorless cameras. It covers a wide range of focal lengths from wide-angle to portrait, making it an ideal "walk-around" lens for travel and everyday photography. As part of the prestigious S-Line, it delivers exceptional sharpness and clarity across the entire frame. The lens features a retractable design, making it highly portable without compromising on optical quality, and its constant f/4 aperture ensures consistent exposure at any zoom level.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Standard Zoom (Z-Mount)
  • Aperture: Constant Maximum f/4 (Consistent exposure throughout zoom)
  • Motor: Stepping Motor (STM) for fast, quiet, and precise AF
  • Construction: 14 Elements in 11 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.30m (0.99 ft) across all zoom positions
  • Filter Size: 72mm

Key Features:

  • Constant f/4 Aperture: Unlike cheaper zooms, the aperture doesn't change when you zoom in, providing stable lighting for both photos and video.
  • Nano Crystal Coat: Features advanced anti-reflection coatings that virtually eliminate internal lens element reflections, ghosting, and flare.
  • Weather-Sealed Design: The lens barrel is extensively sealed to protect against dust and moisture, making it reliable for outdoor shooting.
  • Customizable Control Ring: The ring on the lens can be programmed to adjust focus, aperture, or exposure compensation smoothly.
  • S-Line Optics: Being an S-Line lens, it meets the highest standards of resolution and build quality, outperforming many traditional professional lenses.
