Product Name:

Tascam CA-XLR2d-N (Nikon Kit)

Price:

$279

Description:

The Tascam CA-XLR2d-N is a professional-grade XLR microphone adapter designed specifically for Nikon’s mirrorless camera systems. This kit enables filmmakers and videographers to capture high-definition, low-noise audio by connecting professional studio-grade microphones directly to the camera. It utilizes Nikon's accessory shoe to deliver digital audio directly to the camera's processing engine, ensuring a clean signal path. Compact and versatile, it eliminates the need for separate audio recorders, making it an essential tool for high-end video production and interviews.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Nikon Z 9, Z 7II, Z 6II, Z 50, and other compatible Nikon mirrorless cameras
  • A/D Converter: Built-in High-performance High Definition Discrete Architecture (HDDA)
  • Sampling Frequency: 48kHz / 96kHz
  • Bit Depth: 16-bit / 24-bit
  • Input Terminals: 2 x 3-pin XLR/TRS Combo Female Jacks (Mic/Line Level support)
  • Auxiliary Input: 1 x 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack (supports plug-in power)
  • Output: 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack for analog audio out (to camera) or monitoring
  • Weight: Approximately 341g (including shoe adapter)

Key Features:

  • Digital Interface Connection: Transfers digital audio data directly to the camera via the hot shoe, ensuring no signal degradation or background hiss.
  • High-Performance HDDA Preamps: Features Tascam’s signature preamps for transparent, low-noise, and wide dynamic range audio capture.
  • Dual Channel Control: Provides independent gain, level, and phantom power (48V) controls for two separate microphones or line-in sources.
  • Cold Shoe Mount: Includes an extra cold shoe on top of the unit to mount accessories like wireless receivers or LED lights.
  • No Internal Batteries Needed: Power is supplied by the camera through the compatible accessory shoe, streamlining your gear setup.
  • Integrated Cable Management: Designed to keep your camera rig clean and organized without messy external wiring.
