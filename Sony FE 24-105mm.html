Product Name:

Sony FE 24-105mm f/4 G OSS

Price:

$1,298

Description:

The Sony FE 24-105mm f/4 G OSS is a high-performance, all-in-one zoom lens designed for Sony E-mount mirrorless cameras. Covering a versatile range from wide-angle to short-telephoto, this lens is a favorite for travel, event, and documentary photographers. It maintains a constant f/4 maximum aperture throughout the zoom range, ensuring consistent exposure settings. Built with advanced optical elements, it delivers exceptional corner-to-corner sharpness while minimizing chromatic aberrations. The integrated Optical SteadyShot (OSS) image stabilization makes it perfect for handheld shooting, providing crisp images and steady video footage even in challenging lighting conditions.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Standard Zoom Lens
  • Aperture: Constant f/4 (Consistent exposure throughout zoom)
  • Motor: Direct Drive SSM (Super Sonic wave Motor) for fast, quiet AF
  • Construction: 17 Elements in 14 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.38m (1.25 ft)
  • Filter Size: 77mm

Key Features:

  • Advanced Optics: Features four aspherical elements and three ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass elements to suppress distortion and color fringing.
  • Optical SteadyShot (OSS): Built-in image stabilization significantly reduces camera shake for sharper handheld images and smoother video.
  • Fluorine Coating: The front lens element features a fluorine coating that repels water, oil, and fingerprints, making it easier to clean.
  • Customizable Focus Hold Button: A conveniently placed button on the lens barrel allows you to lock focus or assign other functions via the camera menu.
  • Dust and Moisture Resistant: Designed with seals to protect against harsh weather conditions, making it reliable for outdoor professional use.
  • Compact and Lightweight: Despite its wide zoom range, it weighs only about 663g, making it highly portable for all-day shooting.
