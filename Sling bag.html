Product Name:

Sling Bag SL100

Price:

$530

Description:

The Sling Bag SL100 is an innovative carrying solution designed for photographers who need quick and easy access to their gear while on the move. Unlike traditional backpacks, the sling design allows you to swing the bag to your front without taking it off, ensuring you never miss a shot. It is crafted with weather-resistant materials and features a ergonomic strap that distributes weight evenly across your shoulder for all-day comfort during long street photography sessions.


Specifications:

  • Capacity: Fits one DSLR/Mirrorless body and 2 small lenses.
  • Design: Single-strap sling design for rapid rotation.
  • Material: High-density water-repellent nylon fabric.
  • Tablet Slot: Dedicated padded compartment for 7-inch tablets.
  • Tripod Holder: Side attachment straps for compact travel tripods.

Key Features:

  • Quick Access: Side-entry door allows for camera retrieval in seconds.
  • Breathable Mesh: Back panel features air-mesh for ventilation.
  • Custom Interior: Padded Velcro dividers can be reshaped easily.
  • Hidden Pocket: Secure rear pocket for passports and cash.
  • Rain Cover: Includes a stowaway waterproof cover for extreme weather.
