Product Name:

ME-D10

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon ME-D10 is a high-quality, compact digital microphone designed specifically for Nikon Z series and other compatible digital cameras. It captures superior, crystal-clear sound compared to a camera's built-in mic, making it perfect for vloggers, travelers, and content creators. Its directional pickup pattern focuses on the subject in front of the lens while suppressing unwanted background noise, ensuring your video's audio matches its visual quality.


Specifications:

  • Type: Back-electret condenser microphone
  • Frequency Response: 70Hz to 16,000Hz
  • Sensitivity: -42 dB (0 dB=1V/Pa, 1kHz)
  • Interface: Built-in 3.5mm Gold-plated Stereo Mini-jack
  • Output Impedance: 2.2 kOhms or less
  • Weight: Approximately 92g (3.2 oz)
  • Dimensions: 38 x 79 x 87 mm (1.5 x 3.1 x 3.4 in)

Key Features:

  • Directional Pickup: Features a super-cardioid pattern that emphasizes sound coming from directly in front of the camera, ideal for interviews and vlogging.
  • Built-in Vibration Isolation: Equipped with an integrated shock mount that minimizes mechanical noise from the camera and lens during operation.
  • No Battery Required: It uses "Plug-in Power," drawing energy directly from the camera’s microphone jack, so you never have to worry about charging it.
  • Wind Noise Reduction: Comes with a dedicated furry windscreen (Deadcat) to ensure clean recordings even in breezy outdoor conditions.
  • Compact & Lightweight: Specifically designed to be portable without obstructing the camera's viewfinder or flash.
  • Durable Build: High-quality construction that withstands the rigors of field use while maintaining a professional aesthetic.
