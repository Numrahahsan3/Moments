Product Name:

Sony ECM-LV1 Stereo Lavalier Microphone

Price:

$30

Description:

The Sony ECM-LV1 is a high-quality, compact stereo lavalier microphone designed for clear and natural sound reproduction. It is an ideal choice for content creators, vloggers, and professionals who need a low-profile audio solution that doesn't compromise on quality. Unlike standard mono lavalier mics, the ECM-LV1 captures stereo audio, providing a more immersive and realistic soundscape. Its omni-directional capsules are designed to pick up sound from all directions, making it forgiving during movement. The microphone is incredibly versatile, featuring a 360-degree rotating clip that allows for flexible attachment to clothing at any angle, ensuring the mic stays close to the source while remaining discreetly out of view.

Specifications:

  • Microphone Type: Electret Condenser (Stereo)
  • Frequency Response: 50 Hz to 15,000 Hz
  • Pickup Pattern: Omni-directional (Stereo)
  • Connector: 3.5mm Stereo Mini Plug (Gold-plated)
  • Power Source: Plug-in Power (Supplied by the recording device)
  • Cable Length: Approx. 1.0 m (3.3 ft)
  • Weight: Approximately 12g (0.4 oz) including cable and clip

Key Features:

  • High-Quality Stereo Sound: Equipped with two microphone capsules to deliver clear stereo audio, perfect for capturing natural environment sounds and dialogue.
  • 360-Degree Rotating Clip: The integrated clip rotates freely, allowing you to attach the mic horizontally on a tie or vertically on a pocket without straining the cable.
  • Plug-in Power System: No batteries required! The microphone draws its operating power directly from your camera, smartphone, or voice recorder.
  • Windscreen Included: Comes with a dedicated foam windscreen that effectively reduces pops and wind noise when recording outdoors or in breezy conditions.
  • Compact & Discrete: Small enough to be almost invisible on camera, making it perfect for interviews, YouTube videos, and professional presentations.
