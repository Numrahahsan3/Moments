Product Name:

R1C1 Wireless Close-Up Speedlight System

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon R1C1 Wireless Close-Up Speedlight System is a complete lighting solution specifically designed for Macro and Close-up photography. This system includes two SB-R200 Remote Speedlights and one SU-800 Wireless Speedlight Commander. It mounts directly to the front of the lens, providing excellent shadow-free lighting for subjects like flowers, insects, and dental photography. Operating on Nikon's Creative Lighting System (CLS), it allows for independent wireless control of each flash unit without the need for cables.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Nikon i-TTL cameras; optimized for Macro lenses like the 105mm or 60mm Micro-Nikkor.
  • Components: Includes 2x SB-R200 Speedlights and 1x SU-800 Wireless Commander.
  • Transmission Range: Approx. 20m (66 ft.) for SU-800; uses infrared wireless communication.
  • Power Source: Each SB-R200 and the SU-800 uses one CR123A lithium battery.
  • Weight: SB-R200 (approx. 120g each); SU-800 (approx. 160g).
  • Mounting: SX-1 Attachment Ring (included) supports up to 8 SB-R200 units.

Key Features:

  • Precision Wireless Control: Through the SU-800 Commander, you can control the power of each flash unit directly from the camera without any cables.
  • Flexible Positioning: The SB-R200 units can be rotated anywhere on the lens ring or placed separately on a tripod for creative lighting.
  • Shadow-Free Macro: The circular lighting setup around the lens eliminates harsh shadows, providing soft and even illumination.
  • Target Light: Each SB-R200 features a built-in LED target light to assist with accurate focusing in low-light conditions.
  • Extreme Close-Up Support: Includes an Extreme Close-Up Position Adapter that directs light specifically toward objects positioned very close to the lens.
  • Color Filter System: Comes with a set of colored filters for creative lighting effects and precise white balance adjustments.
