Product Name:

AF-S 50mm f/1.8G

Price:

$279

Description:

The AF-S Nikkor 50mm f/1.8G is a must-have prime lens for Nikon DSLR users, often celebrated as the ultimate "Nifty Fifty" for the F-mount system. It features a fast maximum aperture of f/1.8, which excels in low-light conditions and produces a shallow depth of field for stunning background blur (bokeh). This lens is equipped with Nikon’s Silent Wave Motor (SWM), ensuring fast, accurate, and quiet autofocus, making it a versatile choice for everything from everyday snapshots to high-quality portraiture.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Fixed Focal Length)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 (Excellent for low light and bokeh)
  • Motor: SWM (Silent Wave Motor) for fast and quiet autofocus
  • Construction: 7 Elements in 6 Groups (includes 1 Aspherical element)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.45m (1.48 ft)
  • Filter Size: 58mm

Key Features:

  • M/A Focus Mode Switch: Allows for seamless switching between automatic and manual focusing by simply turning the focus ring, providing instant manual override.
  • Aspherical Lens Element: Incorporates an aspherical element to virtually eliminate coma and other types of aberrations, even when shooting at the widest aperture.
  • Weather-Sealed Mount: Features a rubber gasket around the metal lens mount to protect against dust and moisture, ensuring reliability in outdoor conditions.
  • Super Integrated Coating (SIC): Nikon's specialized coating enhances light transmission efficiency and offers superior color consistency while reducing flare and ghosting.
  • Nikon F-Mount Compatibility: Fully compatible with both FX (Full-Frame) and DX (Crop-Sensor) Nikon DSLRs. On DX cameras, it provides a 75mm equivalent field of view, which is perfect for tight portraits.
