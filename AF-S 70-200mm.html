Product Name:

AF-S 70-200mm f/2.8E FL ED VR

Price:

$279

Description:

The AF-S NIKKOR 70-200mm f/2.8E FL ED VR is a legendary telephoto zoom lens, favored by professionals for sports, wildlife, and portrait photography. It features a constant f/2.8 aperture that provides exceptional low-light performance and consistent exposure across the entire zoom range. This "E" version includes an Electromagnetic Diaphragm for stable auto-exposure during high-speed bursts and advanced Fluorite (FL) lens elements that significantly reduce weight while enhancing optical clarity.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Telephoto Zoom (FX Format)
  • Aperture: Constant Maximum f/2.8 (Ideal for low light and bokeh)
  • Vibration Reduction (VR): Up to 4 stops of image stabilization
  • Construction: 22 Elements in 18 Groups (including 6 ED glass elements)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 1.1m (3.61 ft) throughout the zoom range
  • Filter Size: 77mm

Key Features:

  • Next-Gen Vibration Reduction (VR): Provides up to 4.0 stops of stabilization and includes a 'Sport' mode for tracking fast-moving subjects.
  • Fluorite (FL) and Magnesium Alloy: The use of Fluorite glass elements makes the lens lighter and better balanced than its predecessors without compromising quality.
  • Weather-Sealed Design: Professional-grade dust and moisture resistance, with a fluorine coating that repels water, fingerprints, and dirt.
  • Silent Wave Motor (SWM): Delivers ultra-fast, near-silent autofocus with full-time manual focus override for precise control.
  • Nano Crystal Coat: Virtually eliminates internal reflections, flare, and ghosting, even in harsh backlighting conditions.
  • Customizable Function Buttons: Four focus-lock buttons on the lens barrel allow for quick access to specific settings during shooting.
