Product Name:

Sony XLR-K3M Digital XLR Adapter Kit

Price:

$598

Description:

The Sony XLR-K3M is a professional dual-channel digital XLR audio adapter and microphone kit designed for Sony Alpha series and Cinema Line cameras. This versatile adapter allows you to connect high-quality external microphones and line-level sources via two XLR/TRS combo connectors and an additional 3.5mm stereo mini jack. It features an integrated digital audio interface through the Multi Interface (MI) Shoe, ensuring a noise-free, high-speed audio stream directly into the camera. With its robust, vibration-resistant design and comprehensive manual controls, it is the perfect solution for wedding videographers, documentary filmmakers, and content creators who require broadcast-standard audio without the clutter of extra cables or external power sources.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Sony Alpha a7R IV, a7S III, a9 II, a1, FX3, and FX6 (via MI Shoe)
  • Inputs: 2 x XLR/TRS Combo Jacks, 1 x 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack
  • Audio Interface: Digital (via MI Shoe) or Analog (switchable)
  • Sampling Frequency: 48 kHz / 24-bit (Digital Mode)
  • Frequency Response: 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz
  • Weight: Approx. 187g (Adapter unit only)
  • Dimensions: 116.5 x 113.2 x 119.2 mm

Key Features:

  • Digital Audio Interface: Delivers pure, low-noise audio by skipping the camera's internal analog circuitry, providing a clean signal directly through the Multi Interface Shoe.
  • Dual XLR/TRS Combo Inputs: Features two independent combo terminals that support both XLR microphones and 1/4" TRS jacks for instruments or line sources.
  • Comprehensive Manual Control: Includes dedicated dials and switches for Gain, Low-cut Filter, and ATT (Attenuator) for each channel under a protective clear cover.
  • Supplied Shotgun Microphone: Comes bundled with the ECM-XM1 super-cardioid shotgun microphone, ready for high-quality mono recording out of the box.
  • No Batteries Required: Conveniently draws power directly from the camera body via the MI Shoe, reducing setup weight and complexity.
