Product Name:

ProGrade Digital SDXC (UHS-II)

Price:

$279

Description:

The ProGrade Digital SDXC UHS-II V90 memory card is a premium storage solution engineered for professional photographers and cinema-grade videographers. This high-performance card is specifically designed to handle the massive data flow required for 4K, 6K, and 8K video recording. Utilizing the latest UHS-II technology, it ensures that your camera's buffer clears almost instantly during rapid-fire burst shooting. With its rugged construction, it is built to perform in extreme professional environments while ensuring the safety of your high-resolution digital assets.


Specifications

  • Card Type: SDXC (Secure Digital High Capacity)
  • Bus Interface: UHS-II (Ultra High Speed Phase II)
  • Storage Capacity: Available from 64GB up to 512GB
  • Video Speed Class: V90 (Minimum 90MB/s sustained video capture)
  • UHS Speed Class: U3 (Supports 4K and 8K UHD video recording)
  • Operating Temperature: -13°F to 185°F (-25°C to 85°C)

Key Features:

  • Extreme Transfer Speeds: With read speeds of up to 300MB/s, you can transfer large files from the camera to your computer in seconds, significantly speeding up your editing workflow.
  • Cinema-Quality Video Capture: Rated V90 and UHS Speed Class 3 (U3), this card is capable of capturing uninterrupted, stutter-free 8K, 4K, and Full HD cinema-quality video.
  • Durability & Protection: Built to withstand the toughest conditions; it is shockproof, temperature-proof, waterproof, and even X-ray proof, ensuring your life's work is protected.
  • RescuePRO Deluxe Software: Comes with a downloadable RescuePRO Deluxe data recovery software offer, helping you recover images that were accidentally deleted.
  • Backwards Compatibility: While optimized for UHS-II cameras, it is backwards compatible with UHS-I devices, performing at the maximum speeds supported by the camera's bus architecture.
