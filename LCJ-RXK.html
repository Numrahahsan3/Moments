Product Name:

LCJ-RXK Jacket Case

Price:

$85

Description:

The Sony LCJ-RXK is a premium, stylish jacket-style carrying case designed exclusively for the RX100 series cameras. Crafted with a high-quality leather-like finish, this case provides robust protection against scratches, dust, and minor impacts while maintaining the sleek aesthetic of your premium compact camera. Its thoughtful two-piece design allows photographers to quickly flip the cover and start shooting without ever removing the camera from the base of the case. Whether you are traveling through the city or shooting on location, this case offers the perfect blend of vintage style and modern functionality, ensuring your gear stays safe and ready for the perfect shot.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Sony RX100, RX100 II, RX100 III, RX100 IV, RX100 V, RX100 VI, and RX100 VII
  • Material: Polyurethane (High-quality leather-like finish)
  • Attachment: Secure 1/4"-20 Tripod Screw Mount
  • Includes: Jacket Case (Base + Cover) and Matching Shoulder Strap
  • Color Options: Available in Black and Beige
  • Weight: Approximately 115g (4.1 oz)
  • Dimensions: 11.5 x 8.0 x 5.0 cm (Fits snug to RX100 body)

Key Features:

  • All-in-One Protection: Protects the camera body, lens, and LCD screen from scratches and accidental bumps during transport.
  • Quick Access Design: The flip-open cover allows for immediate shooting, ensuring you never miss a moment while trying to unpack your gear.
  • Full Functionality: Designed to allow access to the USB port and tripod mount even while the base of the case is attached to the camera.
  • LCD Tilting Support: The case is specifically shaped to allow for the tilting of the camera's LCD screen without interference.
  • Premium Shoulder Strap: Comes with a matching, adjustable shoulder strap for comfortable and secure carrying throughout the day.
