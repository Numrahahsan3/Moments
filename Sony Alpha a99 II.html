Description:

The Sony Alpha a99 II is a professional-grade flagship DSLR featuring Translucent Mirror Technology. It is built for high-speed performance and incredible resolution, making it a powerhouse for commercial photographers and action shooters. With its rugged design and advanced autofocus, it represents the pinnacle of Sony's A-mount camera lineup.


Specifications:

  • Sensor: 42.4 MP Full-Frame Exmor R CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: BIONZ X Image Processor & Front-End LSI
  • Video: Internal UHD 4K Video & S-Log3 Gamma
  • Autofocus: 79-Point Hybrid Cross AF System
  • Display: 3.0-inch 1,228k-Dot Tilting LCD

Key Features:

  • 42.4 Megapixel Full-Frame Sensor: Back-illuminated sensor provides stunning resolution and low-noise performance.
  • 12 fps Continuous Shooting: Fast burst shooting with full AF/AE tracking to capture high-speed action.

  • Rugged Magnesium Alloy Body: Dust and moisture-resistant design built for professional use in tough environments.
