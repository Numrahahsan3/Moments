Product Name:

Sony Alpha a68 II

Price:

$650

Description:

The Sony Alpha a68 II is a high-performance DSLR featuring Translucent Mirror Technology, engineered for lightning-fast autofocus and professional-grade clarity. It is the perfect choice for action photographers and hybrid creators who need a reliable system for both high-speed sports shooting and cinematic video production.


Specifications:

  • Sensor: 24.2 MP APS-C Exmor CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: BIONZ X Image Processor
  • AF Points: 79-Point Phase-Detection AF System
  • Video: Full HD 1080p at 60/30/24 fps (XAVC S)
  • Display: 2.7-inch Tiltable TFT LCD Screen

Key Features:

  • 4D Focus Technology: Delivers wide autofocus coverage and fast tracking, ensuring moving subjects stay in sharp focus.
  • SteadyShot INSIDE: In-body image stabilization that works with any compatible A-mount lens to reduce camera shake.
  • OLED Tru-Finder: A high-contrast electronic viewfinder that provides real-time preview of exposure and white balance.
  • 8 fps Continuous Shooting: Capture high-speed action with up to 8 frames per second in Tele-zoom Continuous Advance Priority AE mode.
  • Superior Low Light: Broad ISO range of 100-25600 for clean images with reduced noise in challenging lighting environments.
