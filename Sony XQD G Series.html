Product Name:

Sony XQD G Series

Price:

$279

Description:

The Sony XQD G Series Card is a high-performance media solution designed for professional camcorders and high-end DSLRs. Engineered for dependable 4K video recording and continuous RAW burst photography, this card provides the stability that professional broadcasters and filmmakers rely on. Its robust shell and recessed pins offer superior protection against physical damage, ensuring that your footage remains safe even under the most demanding shooting conditions.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Ideal for Sony FS7, Z280, and compatible Nikon DSLRs
  • Bus Interface: PCIe Gen2 for stable and high-speed data flow
  • Read Speed: Up to 440 MB/s for quick data transfer to PC
  • Write Speed: Up to 400 MB/s for stable 4K video and RAW bursts
  • Capacity Range: Available from 32GB up to 240GB for long shoots
  • Durability: Extremely rigid shell to prevent flexing and bending

Key Features:

  • Dependable 4K Recording: Optimized communication between card and camera allows for consistent, high-quality video capture.
  • RAW Burst Performance: Enables cameras to capture up to 200 frames in a single RAW continuous burst.
  • Robust Shell: Sony’s unique technology increases shell thickness, making it much more durable than standard XQD cards.
  • Recessed Pins: Protects the contact pins from dust, damage, or electrostatic shock during insertion.
  • Workflow Speed: Dramatically cuts down the time needed to back up large video files.
  • Reliable in Extremes: Tested for temperature resistance and airport X-ray safety.
