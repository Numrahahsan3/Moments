Product Name:

Canon GP-E2 GPS Receiver

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon GP-E2 GPS Receiver is a highly functional accessory designed for travel, landscape, and outdoor photographers who want to track their journey accurately. By mounting onto the camera's hot shoe or connecting via USB, it automatically embeds location data—including latitude, longitude, and altitude—directly into the image's EXIF data. It also features a built-in electronic compass that records the camera's orientation. Even when not attached to a camera, it can function as a standalone logger to map out your entire photography route, which can later be synced with your images using Canon software.


Specifications

  • Geotagging Capability: Automatically embeds GPS location information (Latitude, Longitude, Altitude, and UTC time) into the metadata of every photo and video file as you shoot.
  • Electronic Compass : Features an integrated digital compass that records the exact direction the camera was pointing at the time of exposure, providing a more detailed record of your shoot
  • Logging Function: Includes a standalone logging mode that can record your movement path at specific intervals, allowing you to view your entire travel route on a map using "Map Utility" software
  • Power Source : Operates independently on one AA alkaline battery, ensuring that it does not drain your camera’s main battery during long shooting sessions
  • Build & Portability : Designed with a compact and rugged build that weighs only approximately 81g, making it easy to carry without adding significant bulk to your camera setup.

Key Features:

  • Precision Geotagging: Yeh receiver images ki EXIF metadata mein Latitude, Longitude aur Altitude auto-embed karta hai, jis se aapka travel record hamesha save rehta hai.
  • Digital Compass Integration: Ismein built-in electronic compass hai jo image ke saath camera ki orientation (direction) ko bhi record karta hai, jo landscape photography mein bahut kaam aata hai.
  • Standalone GPS Logging: Agar yeh camera se connect na bhi ho, tab bhi yeh aapka poora route map kar sakta hai. Is data ko baad mein Canon ke "Map Utility" software ke zariye images se sync kiya ja sakta hai.
  • Independent Power Supply: Yeh accessory sirf ek **AA Alkaline battery** par chalti hai, iska matlab hai ke yeh aapke camera ki battery bilkul bhi istemal nahi karti, jo long-duration shooting ke liye best hai.
  • Versatile Connectivity: Ise aap camera ke hot-shoe par mount kar sakte hain ya phir USB cable ke zariye connect kar sakte hain, jo ise different Canon EOS models ke saath compatible banata hai.
  • Time Synchronization: Yeh GPS satellite se atomic time data lekar aapke camera ka internal clock auto-set kar deta hai, taake multiple cameras ka time hamesha perfect sync rahe.
