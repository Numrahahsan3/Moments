LCS-SC8 Soft Carrying Case

Price:

$45

Description:

The Sony LCS-SC8 is a premium, heavy-duty soft carrying case specifically engineered to provide maximum protection and organization for your Alpha DSLR or Mirrorless camera setup. Designed for professional photographers and enthusiasts alike, this case features a spacious interior with customizable padded dividers that allow you to snugly fit a camera body with a lens attached, plus 1-2 additional lenses or a flash unit. The rugged polyester construction ensures long-term durability, while the multiple accessory pockets provide dedicated space for memory cards, spare batteries, and filters. Its stylish yet discreet design makes it perfect for travel, street photography, and professional shoots where quick access and equipment safety are top priorities.


Specifications:

  • Material: Durable Polyurethane and Polyester
  • Capacity: Camera body, 2-3 Lenses, Flash, and Accessories
  • Closure Type: Quick-release buckles and Zippered lid
  • Internal Dividers: 3 Moveable padded partitions
  • Carrying Options: Padded shoulder strap and Top handle
  • Weight: Approximately 520g (18.4 oz)
  • External Dimensions: 40 x 24 x 21 cm (15.7 x 9.4 x 8.3 in)

Key Features:

  • Customizable Layout: Features adjustable inner dividers that allow you to reconfigure the interior space to perfectly fit your specific camera gear and lenses.
  • Quick Access Design: Equipped with easy-to-open front buckles, allowing photographers to grab their camera instantly without missing a crucial shot.
  • Extensive Storage: Includes multiple side, front, and mesh pockets for organized storage of small items like lens caps, cleaning kits, and cables.
  • Enhanced Protection: The thick padding absorbs shocks and vibrations, protecting sensitive optics and electronics from accidental bumps during transit.
  • Weather Resistant: Constructed with water-resistant materials to keep your expensive equipment dry and safe from light rain and dust.
