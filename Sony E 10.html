Product Name:

Sony E 10-18mm f/4 OSS

Price:

$849

Description:

The Sony E 10-18mm f/4 OSS is a premium ultra-wide-angle zoom lens specifically designed for Sony APS-C mirrorless cameras. It offers a 35mm equivalent focal length range of 15-27mm, making it an ideal choice for expansive landscapes, architectural photography, and interior shots where space is limited. With a constant maximum aperture of f/4 throughout the entire zoom range, it ensures consistent exposure and control over depth of field. The lens is surprisingly lightweight and compact, perfect for travel and gimbal use. It incorporates Optical SteadyShot (OSS) image stabilization, which helps to minimize camera shake even in challenging low-light conditions or when shooting handheld video.

Specifications

  • Lens Type: Ultra-Wide Angle Zoom
  • Aperture: Constant f/4 Maximum Aperture
  • Mount: Sony E-mount (APS-C)
  • Construction: 10 Elements in 8 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.25m (0.82 ft)
  • Filter Size: 62mm

Key Features:

  • Optical SteadyShot (OSS): Built-in image stabilization compensates for blur caused by camera shake during handheld shooting.
  • Constant f/4 Aperture: Maintain consistent exposure settings as you zoom in and out without needing to adjust ISO or shutter speed.
  • Super ED Glass: Features Super Extra-low Dispersion glass to reduce chromatic aberrations and provide high contrast even at the widest focal lengths.
  • Internal Focusing: Only the middle groups of the optical system move to achieve focus, ensuring the lens length doesn't change during use.
  • Circular Aperture: 7-blade circular diaphragm helps create smoother, more natural-looking rounded bokeh in out-of-focus areas.
