Product Name:

Nikon Lens Pen (Cleaning Kit)

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon Lens Pen is a highly effective and portable cleaning tool designed to keep your camera lenses, binoculars, and scopes free from smudges and fingerprints. It features a unique non-liquid carbon cleaning compound that won't spill or dry out, making it a safe and essential accessory for any photographer's kit. Its compact, pen-like design allows for precision cleaning even in the tightest corners of the lens element.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Safe for all optical lenses, including multi-coated surfaces and filters
  • Cleaning Agent: Proprietary dry carbon compound (no liquids or harsh chemicals required)
  • Brush Type: Retractable high-quality soft goat hair brush for dust removal
  • Cap System: Self-replenishing tip design that recharges with carbon every time the cap is twisted on
  • Weight: Extremely portable, weighing approximately 15g
  • Dimensions: Pen-shaped design, approximately 12.5 cm in length

Key Features:

  • Dual-Ended Tool: Features a retractable brush on one end for loose dust and a circular cleaning tip on the other for stubborn smudges.
  • No Messy Liquids: Uses a dry carbon cleaning system, eliminating the need for sprays, wipes, or tissues that can leave streaks.
  • Long-Lasting Performance: Designed for up to 500 cleanings without losing its effectiveness.
  • Safe for Optics: The soft cleaning tip and brush are designed to remove oil and grime without scratching delicate lens coatings.
  • Pocket-Sized Convenience: Comes with a handy clip, making it easy to carry in a shirt pocket or a small camera bag side slot.
