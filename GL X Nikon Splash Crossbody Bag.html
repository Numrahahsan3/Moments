Product Name:

GL X Nikon Splash Crossbody Bag

Price:

$279

Description:

The GL X Nikon Splash Crossbody Bag is an exclusive and limited-edition collaboration that offers a perfect blend of style and functionality. This bag is designed for photographers who want to keep their gear safe while navigating the city and look trendy at the same time. Its compact design and splash-resistant material make it ideal for all weather conditions. This bag is specifically designed for mirrorless cameras like the Nikon Z series.


Specifications

  • Capacity: Suitable for one mirrorless camera body and one medium-sized lens.
  • Material: Premium Splash-resistant Nylon that protects gear from light rain and dust.
  • Strap Type: Adjustable heavy-duty crossbody strap featuring "GL X Nikon" branding.
  • Closure: High-quality YKK zippers that are smooth and durable.
  • Weight: Extremely lightweight (approx. 300g), making it comfortable for all-day wear.
  • Dimensions: Approximately 21 x 11 x 16 cm (Compact layout).

Key Features:

  • Splash-Proof Design: Its material prevents water droplets from penetrating inside, which is essential for outdoor shoots.
  • Quick Access: Its crossbody design allows you instant access to your camera so you never miss a shot.
  • Iconic Branding: The bag features the signature Nikon and GL logos, making it a premium collector's item.
  • Soft Inner Lining: The interior is made of soft fabric that prevents scratches on the camera body.
  • Versatile Use: In the absence of a camera, it can also be used as a stylish daily-use travel bag.
