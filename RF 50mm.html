Product Name:

RF 50mm f1.8 STM

Price:

$645

Description:

The RF 50mm f/1.8 STM is a compact and affordable prime lens designed specifically for Canon’s full-frame mirrorless R-series cameras. Continuing the legacy of the famous "Nifty Fifty," this lens features a bright f/1.8 aperture, making it excellent for low-light photography and creating a shallow depth-of-field with beautiful background blur. It is equipped with a Stepping Motor (STM) for quiet and smooth autofocus, which is ideal for both high-quality stills and video work. Its lightweight design and the addition of a customizable control ring—allowing users to adjust settings like ISO or aperture directly on the lens—make it a versatile and essential tool for everyday shooting and portraiture.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Standard)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 (Minimum f/22)
  • Motor: STM (Stepping Motor)
  • Construction: 6 Elements in 5 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.30m (0.98 ft)
  • Filter Size: 43mm

Key Features:

  • Customizable Ring:The lens features a control ring that allows you to directly adjust settings like Aperture, ISO, Shutter Speed, or Exposure Compensation with a twist of your fingers.
  • Dual Function:A switch on the side lets you toggle this ring between "Focus" (for manual focusing) and "Control" (for camera settings).
  • Closer Focus:The RF version can focus at a closer distance of 30 cm (0.98 ft) compared to the 35 cm of the EF version.
  • Higher Magnification:It offers a 0.25x magnification, making it better for close-up shots of food, flowers, or small details.
  • No Adapter Needed:It mounts directly onto EOS R-series bodies (like the R6, R7, R10, R50, etc.). You don't need the bulky EF-EOS R adapter.
