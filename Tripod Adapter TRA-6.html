Product Name:

Tripod Adapter TRA-6

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon TRA-6 Tripod Adapter is a high-quality accessory designed to bridge the gap between heavy-duty binoculars and professional tripods. It allows users to securely mount large or high-magnification binoculars onto a standard tripod, eliminating the handshake and vibration that often occur during handheld viewing. This is especially beneficial for birdwatchers, stargazers, and wildlife enthusiasts who need a stable and steady image for extended observation periods. Its slim design ensures it fits between the barrels of most compatible binoculars without obstruction.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed for Nikon Monarch, Action, and Action Extreme series binoculars (and other standard tripod-ready models)
  • Mounting Thread: Standard 1/4"-20 tripod socket compatible
  • Construction: Durable, high-grade metal/composite build
  • Installation Type: Screw-in mechanism for binocular bridge
  • Weight: Approximately 45g (Ultra-lightweight)
  • Dimensions: Compact vertical stem design for slim-profile fitting

Key Features:

  • Rock-Steady Viewing: Completely removes image shake, allowing you to see fine details that are impossible to spot while holding binoculars by hand.
  • Slim Profile: Specifically engineered with a narrow stem so it can fit easily between the objective lenses of even compact-bridge binoculars.
  • Easy Installation: Features a simple thumb-screw mechanism that allows you to attach or detach the adapter in seconds without any tools.
  • Universal Base: The bottom is threaded to fit any standard tripod, monopod, or window mount that uses a 1/4-inch screw.
  • Durable Build: Made from high-quality materials to ensure it can support the weight of larger, heavier professional binoculars safely.
