Product Name:

Nikon ML-L7 Bluetooth Remote Control

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon ML-L7 is a compact and lightweight remote control that uses Bluetooth connectivity to allow wireless control over key camera functions. It is an essential tool for photographers and videographers who want to avoid camera shake during long exposures or include themselves in group shots. The remote allows you to trigger the shutter, start/stop video recording, and even control zoom on compatible Nikon cameras from a distance.


Specifications

  • Connectivity: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for reliable wireless communication with minimal battery drain
  • Operating Distance: Effective range of approximately 10 meters (33 feet) in any direction
  • Battery Type: Powered by a single CR2032 3V lithium coin-cell battery
  • Compatibility: Designed for Nikon Z-series mirrorless cameras (e.g., Z50, Z fc, Z30) and select COOLPIX models (e.g., P1000, P950)
  • Weight: Featherlight at approximately 18g (including battery)
  • Dimensions: Slim and portable, approximately 35.0 x 80.0 x 10.0 mm

Key Features:

  • Full Camera Control: Allows for remote shutter release, video start/stop, and adjustment of camera settings like ISO or Aperture.
  • Zoom Functionality: Features dedicated zoom buttons (+/-) to operate the lens zoom on compatible Nikon cameras and lenses.
  • Pair-and-Go: Easy Bluetooth pairing ensures a quick connection without the need for line-of-sight like traditional infrared remotes.
  • Multi-Function Buttons: Includes an 'Fn' button that can be customized to trigger specific camera functions mapped by the user.
  • Tactile Design: The buttons are designed for easy tactile recognition, allowing you to operate the remote by feel while looking at the subject.
