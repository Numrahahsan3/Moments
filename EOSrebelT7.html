Description:

Canon EOS Rebel T7 is a powerful beginner DSLR camera, designed for sharp photography and smooth video recording. Ideal for students, learners, and photography enthusiasts. It is widely considered an ideal starting point for those looking to move beyond smartphone photography into a dedicated system for learning the fundamentals of the craft.


Specifications:

  • Sensor: 24.1 MP APS-C CMOS Sensor
  • Processor:DIGIC 4+ Image Processor
  • Video:Full HD 1080p Video Recording
  • Built-in:Built-in Wi-Fi & NFC
  • Display:3.0-inch LCD Display

Key Features:

  • 24.1 Megapixel APS-C CMOS Sensor: Provides high-resolution images with enough detail for large prints and creative cropping.
  • DIGIC 4+ Image Processor: Ensures smooth performance and helps process images quickly, especially in low-light conditions.
