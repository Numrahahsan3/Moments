Product Name:

Canon BR-E1 Wireless Remote Control

Price:

$530

Description:

Canon BR-E1 aik Bluetooth-enabled wireless remote control hai jo makhsoos Canon EOS cameras ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Ye un photographers aur videographers ke liye behtareen hai jo camera ko hath lagaye baghair door se shutter release, focus control, ya video recording start/stop karna chahte hain. Iski sab se bari khusoosiyat iska 360-degree range hai, jo purane infrared remotes ke muqabla mein zyada reliable hai kyunke iske liye remote aur camera ka aamne-saamne hona zaroori nahi.

Specifications:

  • Connectivity: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) technology ka istemal karta hai jo battery ki bachat aur fast connection faraham karti hai.
  • Operating Range: Taqreeban 16 feet (5 meters) ki doori tak har taraf (360 degrees) se kaam karta hai.
  • Power Source: Aik single CR2032 Lithium button-cell battery par chalta hai jo lambay arsay tak chalti hai.
  • Weight: Nihayat halka aur compact (taqreeban 14 grams), jo ise travel ke liye behtareen banata hai.
  • Compatibility: EOS R series, 6D Mark II, 90D, 800D, 200D, M50, aur deegar modern Bluetooth-enabled Canon cameras ke saath chalta hai.

Key Features:

  • Multi-Function Controls: Is remote se aap shutter release, AF start (focus), aur 2-second delay shutter trigger kar sakte hain.
  • Video Recording: Is mein aik dedicated button hai jis se aap door kharay hokar video recording start ya stop kar sakte hain, jo vlogging ke liye nihayat mufeed hai.
  • Zoom Control: Agar aap Power Zoom Adapter (PZ-E1) istemal kar rahe hain, to aap is remote ke zariye lens ko zoom-in ya zoom-out bhi kar sakte hain.
  • No Line-of-Sight Required: Bluetooth hone ki wajah se ye rukawaton ke peeche se bhi kaam karta hai, yaani aap camera ke peeche kharay hokar bhi ise trigger kar sakte hain.
  • Simple Pairing: Aik baar camera ke saath pair hone ke baad, ye foran connect ho jata hai aur purane remotes ki tarah har baar setup ki zaroorat nahi parti.
