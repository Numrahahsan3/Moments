Product Name:

Speedlite 430EX III-RT

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon Speedlite 430EX III-RT is a versatile, mid-range portable flash that offers a balance of power and simplicity. It is a significant upgrade over previous models because it features Radio Transmission (RT) wireless technology, allowing you to trigger the flash even when it is not in a direct line of sight with the camera. It features a fast recycle time, a tilting and swiveling head for "bounce" lighting, and an intuitive dot-matrix LCD for easy settings adjustment. It is ideal for portrait photographers and enthusiasts who need professional lighting in a compact form factor.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Compatible with all Canon EOS cameras (DSLR and Mirrorless) that feature a standard Hot Shoe
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: It features a Standard Hot Shoe for camera mounting and an Optical/Radio sensor for wireless triggering
  • Weight: Approximately 295g (Body only, without batteries).
  • Dimensions: Approximately 70.5 x 113.8 x 98.2 mm

Key Features:

  • Radio Transmission: Built-in 2.4 GHz radio triggering system jo flash ko camera se door (off-camera) chalane mein madad deta hai.
  • Compact & Powerful: Iska Guide Number 141 ft./43m (ISO 100 par) hai, jo chote size ke bawajood behtareen roshni deta hai.
  • Fast Recycle Time: Ye flash bahut tezi se dobara charge hota hai (0.1 se 3.5 seconds mein) taake aapka koi shot miss na ho.
  • Bounce Versatility: Iska head 0-90° upar tilt hota hai aur 150° left/180° right rotate hota hai, jis se light ko bounce karna asaan hai.
  • Dot-matrix LCD: Isme naya aur saaf LCD panel diya gaya hai jo settings ko andhere mein bhi asani se dikhata hai.
