Product Name:

Sony PCK-LG1 Glass Screen Protector

Price:

$28.00

Description:

The Sony PCK-LG1 is a premium glass screen protector specifically designed to provide high-level protection for the LCD screens of compatible Sony Alpha cameras. Constructed from shatterproof hard glass, it offers a sturdy barrier against accidental impacts, scratches, and deep scuffs without compromising the visual integrity of your display. Its high transparency ensures that colors remain vibrant and touch-screen sensitivity is fully maintained. This essential accessory is perfect for photographers working in rugged environments who need to safeguard their equipment’s most vulnerable component while ensuring a crystal-clear view for composition and playback.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Sony Alpha a9, a7R II, a7R III, a7 III, a7S II, a7 II, and RX10 series
  • Material: Optical Hard Glass (9H Surface Hardness)
  • Coating: Anti-stain and Anti-fingerprint (Oleophobic)
  • Transparency: Approximately 95% Light Transmission
  • Thickness: Ultra-slim profile for seamless fit
  • Touch Support: Fully compatible with touch-panel functions
  • Shatterproof: Special safety layer to prevent shards if broken

Key Features:

  • 9H Hardness Glass: Made from high-quality optical glass that provides superior protection against scratches from keys, sand, and other sharp objects.
  • Fingerprint Resistance: Features a specialized anti-stain coating that repels oil and fingerprints, making it incredibly easy to wipe clean.
  • Preserved Touch Sensitivity: Engineered to ensure that touch-screen operations remain fluid and responsive, just like the original screen.
  • Shatterproof Design: In the event of a severe impact, the glass is designed not to splinter into dangerous pieces, keeping both you and your camera safe.
  • Easy Bubble-Free Installation: The protector adheres smoothly to the LCD without trapping air bubbles, maintaining a professional and clean look.
