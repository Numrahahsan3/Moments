Product Name:

Sony VCT-P300 Tripod

Price:

$249

Description:

The Sony VCT-P300 is a premium, lightweight, and incredibly compact travel tripod designed for photographers and videographers who refuse to compromise on stability. Engineered specifically for high-end mirrorless cameras and DSLRs, this tripod features a unique hexagonal leg design that provides exceptional rigidity while allowing for a highly collapsible frame. It is the perfect companion for landscape photographers and travelers, as it can be folded down to a remarkably small size that fits easily into standard backpacks. Despite its portable nature, the VCT-P300 is built with high-quality materials to ensure it can support heavy professional gear in various outdoor environments.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Mirrorless Cameras, DSLRs, and Compact Camcorders
  • Maximum Height: 158.7 cm (62.5 in)
  • Minimum Height: 36.4 cm (14.3 in)
  • Folded Length: 29.4 cm (11.6 in)
  • Load Capacity: Up to 2.5 kg (5.5 lbs)
  • Weight: Approximately 1.4 kg (3.1 lbs)
  • Leg Sections: 6-section twist lock system

Key Features:

  • Ultra-Compact Folding: The legs fold 180 degrees upwards around the center column, making it one of the most space-efficient tripods in its class.
  • 3-Way Pan-Tilt Head: Includes a versatile head that allows for precise horizontal and vertical positioning, perfect for both stills and video work.
  • Twist-Lock Mechanism: Features a rapid twist-lock system that allows the legs to be extended or retracted in seconds with a simple turn of the wrist.
  • Hexagonal Leg Shape: The specialized hexagonal design reduces vibrations and increases structural strength compared to traditional round legs.
  • Integrated Quick Release: Comes equipped with a quick-release plate for fast mounting and unmounting of your camera gear during fast-paced shoots.
