Product Name:

EOS 90D

Price:

$770

Description:

The Canon EOS 90D is a powerful and versatile DSLR designed for advanced enthusiasts who want high performance for both photography and videography. It features a high-resolution 32.5 Megapixel APS-C CMOS sensor and the fast DIGIC 8 image processor, allowing for impressive detail and high-speed continuous shooting at up to 10 fps. For video creators, it offers uncropped 4K recording at 30p and Dual Pixel CMOS AF for smooth, fast autofocus. With its rugged, weather sealed body and long battery life, the 90D is an excellent choice for capturing sports, wildlife, and everyday high-quality content.


Specifications

  • Sensor: 32.5 Megapixel APS-C CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: DIGIC 8 Image Processor
  • Video: 4K UHD 30p and Full HD 120p
  • Range: ISO 100–25600 (Expandable to 51200)
  • Display: 3.0-inch Vari-angle Touchscreen LCD
  • Storage: SD/SDHC/SDXC Memory Card (UHS-II supported)

Key Features:

  • High-Resolution 32.5MP Sensor: It features the highest resolution ever found in a Canon APS-C (crop sensor) DSLR, allowing for immense detail and flexible cropping.
  • Fast Continuous Shooting: Capable of shooting up to 10 frames per second (fps) through the optical viewfinder and 11 fps in Live View, making it ideal for fast-moving action.
  • Uncropped 4K Video: Unlike many older Canon models, the 90D can record 4K at 30p using the full width of the sensor, preserving your wide-angle perspective.
  • Advanced Dual Pixel CMOS AF: Provides incredibly fast and smooth autofocus during Live View and Video, including Eye Detection AF to keep subjects' eyes in sharp focus.
  • Joystick (Multi-controller): A much-requested physical joystick was added to the back, allowing photographers to shift focus points quickly while looking through the viewfinder.
