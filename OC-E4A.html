Product Name:

OC-E4A Off-Camera Shoe Cord

Price:

$170

Description:

The Canon OC-E4A is a specialized Off-Camera Shoe Cord designed for Canon's latest mirrorless system. Unlike older cords, this is built for the Multi-Function Shoe, allowing you to use accessories like flashes (Speedlites) and microphones away from the camera body while maintaining a high-speed digital connection. It is weather-resistant and draws power directly from the camera, making it ideal for professional off-camera lighting or audio setups.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed for Canon cameras with the Multi-Function Shoe
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: Multi-Function Shoe Connector
  • Weight: Approximately 150g
  • Dimensions: The cord length is approximately 80 cm (2.6 feet) when extended

Key Features:

  • Power Delivery:It can pass power from the camera to the attached accessory, allowing you to use battery-free accessories (like microphones) even when they are not directly mounted on the camera.
  • Multi-Function Shoe Compatibility:This cord is designed to pass high-speed digital data. Unlike older cords, it supports advanced accessories like the EL-5 Speedlite, DM-E1D Microphone, and ST-E10 Transmitter.
  • Distance:The coiled cord typically stretches to allow you to hold a flash or microphone at arm's length or mount it on a flash bracket or tripod nearby.
  • Microphone Relocation:Since it supports the Multi-Function Shoe's digital interface, you can use it to move a digital microphone (like the DM-E1D) off the camera to get it closer to your subject for better audio.
  • Off-Axis Lighting: It allows you to move your flash away from the top of the camera while maintaining full E-TTL II auto-exposure. This helps in creating more natural shadows and avoiding "flat" lighting.
