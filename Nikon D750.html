Product Name:

Nikon D750

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon D750 is a legendary full-frame DSLR known for its remarkable versatility, offering a perfect blend of professional features and a compact, ergonomic design. While the D850 is built for raw power, the D750 is the "workhorse" of the Nikon lineup, beloved by wedding and event photographers for its 24.3-megapixel CMOS sensor that delivers stunning image quality and exceptional low-light performance. Its slim-body design and deep grip make it one of the most comfortable DSLRs to hold for long shooting days, powered by the EXPEED 4 image processor for smooth and reliable operation.


Specifications

  • Sensor: 24.3-megapixel FX-Format (Full-Frame) CMOS sensor
  • Autofocus System: 51-point AF system with 15 cross-type sensors for high-speed accuracy
  • ISO Range: Native ISO 100–12,800 (Expandable to 50–51,200)
  • Build Quality: Monocoque structure with magnesium alloy and carbon fiber; weather-sealed
  • Dimensions: 140.5 x 113 x 78 mm
  • Weight: Approx. 750g (body only)

Key Features:

  • 24.3MP Full-Frame Clarity: Provides the perfect balance between file size and detail, making it ideal for high-volume shooting without filling up hard drives too quickly.
  • Tilting Vari-Angle LCD: The first Nikon full-frame DSLR to feature a tilting 3.2-inch screen, allowing for creative high and low-angle photography.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi: Features integrated Wi-Fi connectivity for seamless image transfer and remote camera control via smartphone.
  • Dual SD Card Slots: Allows for instant backup, overflow storage, or separating RAW and JPEG files for a safer professional workflow.
  • Outstanding Low-Light Performance: Famous for its clean images at high ISOs, making it a go-to choice for dimly lit receptions and indoor events.
  • Full HD Video: Supports 1080p recording at 60/50/30/25/24p with dedicated power aperture control for smooth transitions.
