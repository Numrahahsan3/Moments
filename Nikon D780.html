Product Name:

Nikon D780

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon D780 is a versatile, high-performance DSLR that bridges the gap between traditional photography and modern mirrorless technology. It serves as the successor to the legendary D750, offering a perfect blend of a rugged DSLR build with the advanced autofocus and video capabilities of the Nikon Z series. Equipped with a 24.5MP BSI full-frame sensor and the EXPEED 6 image processor, it delivers exceptional low-light performance and color science. Whether you are shooting through the optical viewfinder or using the improved Live View mode, the D780 provides a fast, reliable, and cinematic experience for both enthusiasts and professionals.


Specifications

  • Sensor: 24.5-megapixel Back-Illuminated (BSI) Full-Frame CMOS sensor.
  • Image Processor: EXPEED 6 (delivering faster processing and better energy efficiency).
  • ISO Range: Native ISO 100–51,200 (expandable up to 204,800).
  • Speed: 7 fps continuous shooting via Viewfinder; up to 12 fps in Silent Live View mode.
  • Dimensions: 143.5 x 115.5 x 76 mm.
  • Build: Weather-sealed magnesium alloy structure for durability in tough conditions.

Key Features:

  • Hybrid AF System: Features a 51-point Phase Detection AF for the viewfinder and a 273-point Focal-plane Phase-Detection AF in Live View (same as the Nikon Z6).
  • Advanced Video Capabilities: Records uncropped 4K UHD video at 30p with N-Log and HDR (HLG) support for professional-grade color grading.
  • Silent Shooting Mode: Allows for completely silent electronic shutter operation in Live View, perfect for weddings or quiet environments.
  • Dual SD Card Slots: Equipped with two UHS-II compatible SD card slots for high-speed data overflow, backup, or file separation.
  • Long Exposure Capabilities: Features an extended shutter speed range (up to 900 seconds) directly in the camera menu without needing a remote.
  • Superb Battery Performance: Can capture approximately 2,260 shots per charge, making it one of the most reliable cameras for long shooting days.
