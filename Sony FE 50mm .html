Product Name:

Sony FE 50mm f/1.8

Price:

$249

Description:

The Sony FE 50mm f/1.8 is a compact, lightweight prime lens designed for Sony's full-frame E-mount mirrorless cameras. Often referred to as a "nifty fifty," this lens is a staple for both beginners and professionals due to its exceptional balance of performance and portability. Its large f/1.8 maximum aperture is perfect for low-light environments and achieving a shallow depth of field, which provides a beautiful, creamy background blur that makes your subject stand out. Whether you are shooting street photography, daily life, or professional portraits, this lens delivers sharp high-quality imagery with minimal aberration, making it a must-have for any Sony Alpha user.

Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Full-Frame E-Mount)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 to Minimum f/22
  • Motor: DC Motor for fast and precise focusing
  • Construction: 6 Elements in 5 Groups (1 Aspherical element)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.45m (1.48 ft)
  • Filter Size: 49mm

Key Features:

  • Compact and Lightweight: Weighing only 186g, it’s incredibly portable and doesn't add bulk to your mirrorless setup.
  • Aspherical Element: Features an aspherical element in its design to control spherical aberrations and maintain sharpness.
  • 7-Blade Circular Aperture: Designed to create a smooth, rounded bokeh effect, giving a natural look to out-of-focus highlights.
  • Solid Build Quality: Features a high-quality metal mount that provides durability and a premium feel.
  • Versatile Focal Length: The 50mm focal length is ideal for a wide range of photography styles, mimicking the field of view of the human eye.
