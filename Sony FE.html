Product Name:

Sony FE 24-70mm f/2.8 GM (G Master)

Price:

$1,998

Description:

The Sony FE 24-70mm f/2.8 GM is the ultimate standard zoom lens designed for professional photographers and serious enthusiasts. As part of Sony’s flagship G Master series, it delivers an incredible balance of high resolution and beautiful bokeh. This lens is a versatile workhorse, covering the most popular focal lengths from wide-angle for landscapes to short telephoto for portraits. With a constant f/2.8 maximum aperture, it maintains consistent performance throughout the zoom range, making it exceptional for low-light conditions and depth-of-field control. Its advanced optical construction ensures clinical sharpness, while the robust weather-sealed design ensures reliability in the toughest shooting environments.

Specifications

  • Lens Type: Standard Zoom Lens
  • Aperture: Constant f/2.8 (Maximum) - f/22 (Minimum)
  • Motor: Direct Drive SSM (Super Sonic wave Motor) for fast, silent AF
  • Construction: 18 Elements in 13 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.38m (1.25 ft)
  • Filter Size: 82mm

Key Features:

  • G Master Resolution: XA (extreme aspherical) element and ED glass provide stunning corner-to-corner resolution and detail.
  • Constant f/2.8 Aperture: Keeps your exposure settings consistent even when zooming, perfect for professional video work.
  • Nano AR Coating: Sony's original Nano AR Coating technology effectively suppresses reflections that can cause flare and ghosting.
  • 9-Blade Circular Aperture: Designed to produce near-perfect circular bokeh for smooth, natural background defocus.
  • Dust and Moisture Resistant: Built with high-grade seals, making it suitable for professional outdoor use in unpredictable weather.
  • Focus Hold Button: Customizable button on the lens barrel for quick access to focus lock or other assigned functions.
