Product Name:

Nikon D3500

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon D3500 is widely celebrated as the ultimate entry-level DSLR, designed specifically for beginners who want to move beyond smartphone photography. It strikes a perfect balance between simplicity and high-quality imaging, featuring a lightweight, ergonomic design that is easy to carry all day. At its core is a 24.2-megapixel APS-C sensor that delivers sharp, vibrant photos with beautiful background blur (bokeh). With its unique "Guide Mode," the D3500 acts as a personal photography tutor, making it the perfect tool for students and hobbyists to learn the ropes of manual shooting.


Specifications

  • Sensor Resolution: 24.2-megapixel APS-C CMOS sensor (No optical low-pass filter for extra sharpness).
  • Image Processor: EXPEED 4 image processing engine.
  • ISO Range: 100–25,600 (Great for low-light versatility).
  • Portability: Compact and lightweight body weighing only approx. 365g.
  • Dimensions: 124 x 97 x 69.5 mm.

Key Features:

  • 24.2MP DX-Format Sensor: Captures incredibly fine detail and produces high-resolution images that are perfect for large prints and social media.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity (SnapBridge): Uses Nikon’s SnapBridge app to automatically send photos to your smartphone or tablet for instant sharing.
  • Incredible Battery Life: One of its strongest selling points, capable of taking up to 1,550 shots per charge—roughly 3x more than many competing mirrorless cameras.
  • Built-in Guide Mode: An interactive on-screen assistant that teaches you how to adjust camera settings to achieve specific photographic effects.
  • 1080p Full HD Video: Records smooth video at 60/50/30/25/24p, providing a cinematic look compared to mobile devices.
  • 11-Point AF System: Features 11 autofocus points spread across the frame to keep subjects sharp, whether they are centered or off-center.
