Product Name:

Sony LCS-BP2 System Backpack

Price:

$159

Description:

The Sony LCS-BP2 is a highly versatile and ergonomic system backpack designed specifically for photographers and videographers who need to carry a full kit comfortably. This professional-grade bag features a unique 3-way carrying system, allowing you to use it as a traditional backpack, or a left/right-handed sling bag for quick access to your gear. Its semi-hard structure provides excellent protection against impacts, while the customizable interior dividers ensure that your Alpha DSLR, SLT, or NEX camera along with multiple lenses and accessories stay securely in place during travel. Whether you are trekking through the city or shooting in the wild, this bag offers the perfect balance of comfort, durability, and accessibility.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: DSLR, SLT, and Mirrorless Cameras with multiple lenses
  • Material: High-quality Polyester and Polyurethane for durability
  • External Dimensions: Approx. 21.0 x 40.0 x 15.0 cm (8.3 x 15.8 x 5.9 in)
  • Carrying Options: Backpack mode or Left/Right Sling mode
  • Interior Color: High-contrast Vermillion (for finding gear in low light)
  • Weight: Approximately 900g (1.9 lbs)
  • Rain Cover: Integrated hidden rain cover for all-weather protection

Key Features:

  • 3-Way Carry System: Easily switch between backpack style and a quick-access sling style by adjusting the straps, perfect for fast-paced shooting.
  • Adjustable Dividers: Includes a set of padded internal dividers that can be repositioned to fit your specific camera body, lenses (up to 70-200mm), and flashes.
  • Dedicated Accessory Pockets: Features multiple side and internal pockets for storing batteries, memory cards, cables, and personal items like a smartphone.
  • Ergonomic Design: Padded shoulder straps and a waist belt help distribute weight evenly, reducing fatigue during long hours of carrying heavy equipment.
  • Quick Side Access: Large side openings allow you to swing the bag around and grab your camera instantly without taking the bag off completely.
