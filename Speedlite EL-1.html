Product Name:

Speedlite EL-1

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon Speedlite EL-1 is a flagship, professional-grade flash designed for photographers who demand extreme reliability and precision. It features a revolutionary xenon flashtube that allows for an incredibly wide power range, from full power down to 1/8192, providing 14 stops of dynamic range. With a built-in active cooling system and a rechargeable lithium-ion battery, it offers ultra-fast recycle times of approximately 0.9 seconds and can handle up to 170 continuous full-power bursts without overheating. It is weather-sealed to the same standard as Canon’s EOS-1D X series, making it the ultimate tool for weddings, sports, and photojournalism.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed for Canon EOS cameras supporting E-TTL II / E-TTL autoflash
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable (This is a light-emitting device, not a sensor/microphone)
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: It features a PC Terminal for wired flash triggering and a Service Terminal for proprietary Canon use
  • Weight: Approximately 572g
  • Dimensions: Approximately 84.4 x 149.0 x 136.4 mm

Key Features:

  • Ultra-Fast Recycle Time: Provides a rapid charging time of approx. 0.1 to 0.9 seconds, ensuring you never miss a shot.
  • Active Cooling System: Features a built-in fan system that allows for continuous firing of up to 170 full-power flashes without overheating.
  • Powerful Battery Performance: Powered by the LP-EL rechargeable Li-ion battery, delivering long-lasting power and high-speed performance.
  • Precision Power Control: Offers a wide power range from full 1/1 down to 1/8192 power for extremely subtle lighting.
  • Weather-Resistant Build: Professional-grade dust and water resistance, matching the durability of Canon EOS-1D series cameras.
  • Modeling Lamp: Equipped with a dual-color LED modeling lamp to help preview light and shadow effects before shooting.
