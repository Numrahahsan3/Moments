Product Name:

Nikon Traveler Bag

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon Traveler Bag is a versatile and rugged messenger-style bag tailored for photographers on the move. Combining a classic aesthetic with modern functionality, it provides ample protection for your Nikon system while maintaining a slim profile. Its weather-resistant exterior and reinforced stitching ensure that your gear remains safe during travel, making it the perfect companion for landscape and travel photographers who need quick access to their equipment.


Specifications

  • Capacity: Spacious enough to accommodate a mid-sized DSLR or Z-series Mirrorless camera, two lenses, and a small tablet or notebook.
  • Storage Pockets: Features a large front zippered compartment for cables and chargers, along with side pouches for quick-access items like lens caps.
  • Material & Durability: Crafted from high-density, tear-resistant canvas with a water-repellent coating to withstand harsh outdoor conditions.
  • Dimensions & Weight: External Size: Approximately 28 x 15 x 22 cm; lightweight design for all-day comfort.
  • Adjustable Dividers: Includes modular, hook-and-loop padded dividers that can be rearranged to secure different body and lens configurations.

Key Features:

  • Rugged Traveler Design: Built with a vintage-inspired look that blends into urban and rural environments seamlessly.
  • Impact Protection: Dense foam walls provide 360-degree shock absorption for sensitive camera sensors and glass.
  • Quick-Access Flap: Designed with a top-access flap and secure buckles, allowing you to grab your camera in seconds when a shot appears.
  • Dedicated Tablet Sleeve: Includes a padded internal sleeve specifically sized for tablets or small documents.
  • Ergonomic Strap: Equipped with a wide, non-slip padded shoulder strap to distribute weight evenly and reduce fatigue.
