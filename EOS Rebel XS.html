Product Name:

EOS Rebel XS

Price:

$1040

Description:

The Canon EOS Rebel XS (also known as the EOS 1000D) is a compact and user-friendly DSLR designed specifically for those entering the world of digital photography. Released as a more affordable alternative in the Rebel lineup, it features a 10.1 Megapixel APS-C CMOS sensor and the DIGIC III image processor, which work together to deliver natural colors and fine detail. While it focuses primarily on still photography and does not support video recording, it offers a lightweight body, a 7-point wide-area autofocus system, and a 2.5-inch LCD monitor with Live View settings. It is a reliable choice for beginners looking to learn manual controls on a budget.


Specifications

  • Sensor: 10.1 Megapixel APS-C CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: DIGIC III Image Processor
  • Video: Not Supported (No Video Recording)
  • Range: ISO 100 – 1600
  • Display: 2.5-inch LCD with Live View
  • Storage: SD and SDHC Memory Card support

Key Features:

  • 10.1 Megapixel CMOS Sensor: Provides high-quality images with enough resolution for large prints and clear details.
  • Live View Function: Unlike many older DSLRs, the XS allows you to compose your photos using the LCD screen instead of just the viewfinder.
  • 7-Point Wide-Area AF: Uses a high-speed autofocus system to quickly lock onto subjects, even if they aren't perfectly centered.
  • DIGIC III Image Processor: Delivers fast operation, accurate colors, and reduced digital noise in shadows.
  • EOS Integrated Cleaning System: Automatically shakes dust off the sensor every time you turn the camera on or off to keep your images clean.
