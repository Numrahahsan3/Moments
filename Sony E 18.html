Product Name:

Sony E 18-135mm f/3.5-5.6 OSS

Price:

$649

Description:

The Sony E 18-135mm f/3.5-5.6 OSS is a highly versatile high-magnification zoom lens designed specifically for APS-C format E-mount mirrorless cameras. Covering a wide range of focal lengths from 18mm wide-angle to 135mm telephoto, this lens is the perfect "all-in-one" solution for travel, street, and landscape photography. Despite its impressive 7.5x zoom range, it maintains a remarkably compact and lightweight design, making it easy to carry all day. The inclusion of Optical SteadyShot (OSS) image stabilization ensures that your handheld photos and videos remain sharp and shake-free, even at the telephoto end or in challenging lighting conditions.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: All-in-One Zoom Lens
  • Aperture Range: f/3.5 - f/5.6 (Maximum) to f/22 - f/36 (Minimum)
  • Mount Type: Sony E-mount (APS-C)
  • Optical Design: 16 Elements in 12 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.45m (1.48 ft)
  • Filter Size: 55mm

Key Features:

  • 7.5x High-Magnification Zoom: Covers everything from wide landscapes to distant subjects with a focal range equivalent to 27-202.5mm in 35mm format.
  • Optical SteadyShot (OSS): Built-in image stabilization compensates for camera shake, delivering crisp images even during handheld shooting.
  • Advanced Optics: Features one aspherical element and two ED (Extra-low Dispersion) glass elements to suppress aberrations and ensure high resolution.
  • Fast and Quiet AF: Equipped with a linear motor that provides rapid, precise, and nearly silent autofocus, making it ideal for both high-speed stills and video.
  • Compact Portability: Weighing only 325g, this lens offers a professional zoom range without the bulk of traditional telephoto lenses.
