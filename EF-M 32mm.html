Product Name:

EF-M 32mm f1.4 STM

Price:

$878

Description:

The EF-M 32mm f/1.4 STM offers a focal length equivalent to 51.2mm on a full-frame camera. This is known as a "normal" perspective, meaning it captures images that look very similar to how the human eye sees the world. Because of its extremely wide f/1.4 aperture, it is the fastest lens in the EF-M lineup, making it a powerhouse for low-light shooting and portrait photography.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Standard Prime Lens
  • Aperture:maximum aperture f/1.4,minimum aperture f/16 hai
  • Motor: STM (Stepping Motor)
  • Construction: 14 elements,8 groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance:0.23m (9.1 inches)
  • Filter Size:43mm

Key Features:

  • High Image Quality: It features a complex design of 14 elements in 8 groups, including a glass-molded aspherical element. This ensures edge-to-edge sharpness and reduces distortions.
  • Super Spectra Coating: This specialized coating prevents lens flare and ghosting, ensuring high contrast and accurate colors even when shooting in bright sunlight.
  • Human-Eye View: With a 51.2mm equivalent focal length, it provides a natural perspective. What you see with your eyes is exactly what you get in the frame, making it highly versatile for daily photography.
  • Shallow Depth of Field: It creates a beautiful, creamy background blur (bokeh) that makes your subject stand out perfectly, which is ideal for professional-looking portraits.
  • Portable: Despite its wide aperture and high-quality glass, it weighs only 235g. It maintains the "compact" philosophy of the EOS M system, making it easy to carry in a small bag all day.
