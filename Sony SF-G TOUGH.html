Product Name:

Sony SF-G TOUGH

Price:

$279

Description:

The Sony SF-G TOUGH Series SDXC Card is the world's first one-piece molded SD card, designed for maximum reliability in the field. This high-performance UHS-II card is engineered to eliminate the fragile parts of a standard SD card—like the write-protect switch and connector ribs—making it virtually indestructible. With industry-leading read and write speeds, it is the perfect companion for professional photographers shooting high-speed sports or unpredictable wildlife.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Compatible with all Sony Alpha, Mirrorless, and DSLR cameras
  • Speed Class: UHS-II, V90, U3, Class 10
  • Read Speed: Up to 300 MB/s for maximum workflow efficiency
  • Write Speed: Up to 299 MB/s for rapid buffer clearing in burst mode
  • Physical Build: One-piece ribless and switchless construction
  • Durability: IP68 Waterproof, Dustproof, and 18x more bend-resistant

Key Features:

  • World's Toughest Design: No fragile ribs or write-protect switches that can break and lock your data.
  • Fastest SD Transfer: Blazing write speeds ensure your camera buffer clears instantly after a high-speed burst.
  • V90 Rated: Guaranteed minimum sustained write speeds of 90MB/s for high-end 4K and 8K video capture.
  • Resilience: Can withstand drops from up to 5 meters and is resistant to mud, dirt, and extreme cold.
  • Safe & Secure: Built to be magnet-proof, anti-static, and X-ray proof for safe travel through airports.
  • Data Recovery: Comes with Sony’s File Rescue software to bring back lost work.
