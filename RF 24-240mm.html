Product Name:

RF 24-240mm f4-6.3 IS USM

Price:

$479

Description:

The RF 24-240mm f/4-6.3 IS USM is a highly versatile, all-in-one zoom lens designed for Canon's full-frame mirrorless system. With its massive 10x optical zoom range, it covers everything from wide-angle landscapes to distant telephoto subjects, making it the perfect "travel lens" for those who want to carry only one lens. It features an advanced Nano USM motor for incredibly fast and silent autofocus, along with Optical Image Stabilization (IS) that provides up to 5 stops of shake correction for sharp photos and smooth video. Its compact design and customizable control ring offer a seamless shooting experience for both photography and videography enthusiasts.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: All-in-One Telephoto Zoom (10x Optical Zoom)
  • Aperture: f/4 to f/6.3
  • Motor: Nano USM (High-speed and Silent)
  • Construction: 21 Elements in 15 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.50m (1.64 ft) at wide end
  • Filter Size: 72mm

Key Features:

  • Ultimate Versatility:This lens covers almost every common focal length. At 24mm, you can capture wide landscapes or group shots; at 240mm, you can zoom in on sports or wildlife.
  • Travel-Friendly:Because it replaces the need for 2 or 3 separate lenses, it significantly reduces the weight of your camera bag.
  • Dynamic IS (for Video):This is Canon's first full-frame lens to feature Dynamic IS, which provides extra stabilization while recording video—perfect for filming while walking.
  • Instant Adjustments:When set to "Control," you can use the ring to quickly adjust settings like Aperture, ISO, or Exposure Compensation directly from the lens.
  • Zoom Lock:Includes a lock switch to prevent "lens creep" (the lens sliding out while hanging by your side).
