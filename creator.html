Creator Accessory Kit

Price:

Description:

The Canon Creator Accessory Kit is a curated bundle designed specifically for content creators, vloggers, and streamers who use Canon EOS R-series cameras. This kit provides the essential tools to stabilize your shots and capture high-quality audio, making it a "one-stop" solution for handheld vlogging or desktop recording. It typically includes a versatile tripod grip with a detachable Bluetooth remote and a compact stereo microphone, allowing you to focus on your story while the gear handles the technical quality.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: This specific accessory kit is designed specifically for Canon EF 70-200mm f/4L, EF 80-200mm f/2.8L, and EF 400mm f/5.6L USM lenses
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Grip only: Approximately 163g
  • Dimensions: Approximately 170.1 x 40.6 x 30.5 mm (when folded as a grip).

Key Features:

  • Complete Vlogging Set: Includes the HG-100TBR Tripod Grip and the DM-E100 Stereo Microphone for a professional setup.
  • Crystal Clear Audio: The lightweight directional microphone plugs directly into your camera for high-quality stereo sound.
  • Seamless Control: Comes with the BR-E1 Wireless Remote for effortless control over recording and zoom functions.
  • Compact Portability: Designed for one-handed operation, making it ideal for travel, daily vlogs, and stable handheld shots.
  • Versatile Mounting: The grip’s head can rotate and tilt, supporting both landscape and vertical video formats easily.
