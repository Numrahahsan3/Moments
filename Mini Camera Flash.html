Universal Mini Camera Speedlite Flash

Price: $45.00

Description:

The Universal Mini Camera Flash is a lightweight and portable lighting solution designed for mirrorless and compact cameras. Despite its small size, it provides a powerful burst of light to eliminate shadows and improve image clarity in low-light indoor or outdoor environments. Perfect for street photography and event snapshots.


Specifications:

  • Guide Number: GN12 (ISO 100, meters)
  • Color Temperature: 5600K ± 200K
  • Recycle Time: Approx. 0.1 - 3.5 seconds
  • Power Source: 2x AAA Batteries
  • Flash Duration: 1/2000s to 1/800s
  • Compatibility: Universal Hot-shoe

Key Features:

  • Ultra-Portable: Slim design that fits easily in your pocket or small gear bag.
  • Plug & Play: Simple one-button operation for quick lighting adjustments.
  • Adjustable Tilt: Bounce light off ceilings for softer, natural-looking shadows.
  • Energy Efficient: Auto-sleep mode to conserve battery life during long shoots.
