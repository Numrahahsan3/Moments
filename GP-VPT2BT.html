Product Name:

Sony GP-VPT2BT Shooting Grip

Price:

$138

Description:

The Sony GP-VPT2BT is a versatile and innovative shooting grip designed for vloggers, travelers, and content creators who use Sony’s mirrorless and compact cameras. This accessory functions as both a stable handheld grip for mobile shooting and a sturdy tabletop tripod for stationary setups. By utilizing Bluetooth wireless technology, it eliminates the need for messy cables, allowing you to control your camera’s essential functions directly from the grip. Its ergonomic design ensures a comfortable hold for long shooting sessions, while its dust and moisture-resistant construction makes it a reliable companion for outdoor adventures. Whether you are recording high-quality video or capturing the perfect selfie, this grip provides the stability and control required for professional-grade results.

Specifications:

  • Connectivity: Bluetooth Standard Ver. 4.2 (2.4 GHz band)
  • Max Load Capacity: 1.5 kg (53.0 oz)
  • Tilting Angle (Grip): Up: 80 degrees, Down: 90 degrees
  • Tilting Angle (Tripod): Up: 40 degrees, Down: 90 degrees
  • Rotation: 360-degree rotation with 90-degree position lock
  • Battery Type: CR2032 Lithium Coin Cell Battery
  • Weight: Approximately 215g (7.6 oz)
  • Dimensions: 49.5 x 173.0 x 42.0 mm (Folded)

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Wireless Remote: No cables needed. You can control shutter release, zoom, and movie recording buttons wirelessly from the grip itself.
  • Quick Tilting Function: Easily adjust the camera angle for various subjects and viewpoints, making it ideal for self-portraits or high/low angle shots.
  • Tripod Conversion: The grip legs can be spread out to transform the unit into a stable mini-tripod, perfect for time-lapses or hands-free vlogging.
  • Contoured Ergonomic Design: Specifically shaped for a secure and comfortable grip, reducing hand fatigue during extended handheld operation.
  • Weather Resistant: Built to complement Sony cameras with dust and moisture resistance, ensuring durability in challenging environments.
