Product Name:

Tripod Mount Ring A

Price:

$279

Description:

The Tripod Mount Ring A (often referred to as the Tripod Mount Ring A II for current versions) is a professional-grade accessory designed to provide better balance and stability when using specific Canon telephoto lenses. Instead of mounting the camera body directly to a tripod, this ring attaches around the barrel of the lens. By shifting the mounting point to the lens's center of gravity, it prevents "lens flop," reduces strain on the camera's lens mount, and allows for seamless switching between horizontal and vertical shooting orientations without removing the camera from the tripod.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed specifically for Canon EF 70-200mm f/4L, EF 80-200mm f/2.8L, and EF 400mm f/5.6L USM lenses.
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Approximately 163g
  • Dimensions: Inner Ring Diameter: Approximately 65mm – 66mm
    Outer Dimensions: Approximately 97 x 73 x 45 mm

Key Features:

  • Perfect Balance: Lens ko tripod par mount karne se camera aur lens ka weight behtar tareeqe se balance hota hai, jis se stability barh jati hai.
  • Vertical & Horizontal Rotation: Ring ko dheela kar ke aap camera ko baghair tripod se hataye Landscape (horizontal) se Portrait (vertical) mode mein aasani se ghuma sakte hain.
  • Secure Locking: Is mein ek mazboot locking knob hota hai jo lens ko apni jagah par fix rakhta hai taake shooting ke dauran lens slip na ho.
  • Anti-Scratch Lining: Ring ke andar narm fabric ya lining hoti hai jo aapke mehngay lens ki body par scratches nahi parne deti.
  • Quick Attachment: Ye ring open-style design ke sath aata hai, yani aap lens ko camera se utare baghair bhi is ring ko charha ya utar sakte hain.
