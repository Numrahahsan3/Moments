Product Name:

Canon RA-E3 Remote Controller Adapter

Price:

$65

Description:

Canon RA-E3 Remote Controller Adapter ek compact aur zaroori accessory hai jo purane E3-type accessories (jaise RS-60E3 remote switch) ko un modern EOS cameras ke saath connect karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai jinmein N3-type socket hota hai. Ye adapter un photographers ke liye nihayat mufeed hai jo naye camera body par shift ho chuke hain lekin apne purane, bharosemand remotes aur triggers ko istemal karna chahte hain. Iska design chota aur halka hai, jo ise travel bag mein rakhne ke liye asaan banata hai.


Specifications:

  • Connector Type: Iska ek end Canon N3-type plug aur doosra end E3-type (2.5mm mini-jack) socket par mushtamil hai.
  • Compatibility: Ye adapter un cameras ke liye hai jinmein 3-pin N3-type remote port hota hai (jaise EOS R5, 5D series, 7D series, aur 1D series).
  • Build: Mazboot aur durable plastic material se bana hai jo field work ke liye behtareen hai.
  • Weight: Nihayat halka (approx. 10-15 grams), jo camera setup par koi wazan nahi dalta.
  • Length: Ye ek short cable adapter hai jo connectivity ke waqt flexibility provide karta hai.

Key Features:

  • Backward Compatibility: Is adapter ki madad se aap apne purane E3-style remote switches aur timer controllers ko naye professional-grade cameras par chala sakte hain.
  • Seamless Connection: Ye baghair kisi signal loss ya delay ke signals transfer karta hai, jo long exposure aur macro photography mein intehayi zaroori hai.
  • Cost Efficient: Naya N3-compatible remote kharidne ke bajaye, ye adapter ek sasta aur behtar hal faraham karta hai.
  • Weather Resistant Design: Iska connector fit itna tight hai ke halki-phulki nami aur dhool se mehfooz rehta hai (agarche ye mukammal waterproof nahi hai).
  • Plug-and-Play: Iske liye kisi software ya battery ki zaroorat nahi hoti; bus plug karein aur istemal shuru karein.
