Product Name:

Nikon D6

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon D6 is the ultimate professional DSLR designed for sports, wildlife, and photojournalists who require absolute reliability and speed. While it features a lower resolution of 20.8-megapixels compared to the D850, it focuses on extreme low-light performance and lightning-fast data processing. Equipped with the EXPEED 6 engine, the D6 is built to deliver clean images in the most challenging lighting conditions. Its rugged, integrated-grip body is designed to be the "workhorse" of the photography world, offering unmatched durability and the most advanced physical connectivity options for instant file sharing in fast-paced environments.


Specifications

  • Professional Sensor: 20.8MP Full-Frame CMOS sensor optimized for speed and low-light ISO performance.
  • Advanced AF Engine: Features a dedicated Triple-Multi-CAM 37K autofocus sensor with 105 all-cross type points.
  • Extreme Speed: Up to 14 fps continuous shooting with full AF/AE tracking.
  • Tank-Like Build: Magnesium alloy chassis with the highest level of dust and moisture resistance in the Nikon lineup.
  • Dimensions: 160 x 163 x 92 mm (Integrated Vertical Grip).

Key Features:

  • 105-Point All-Cross Type AF: Every single focus point is a cross-type sensor, providing incredible density and tracking precision for fast-moving athletes or animals.
  • ISO Range (100 - 102,400): Native ISO goes up to 102,400 (expandable to Hi-5), allowing you to shoot in near-total darkness with usable results.
  • Dual CFexpress/XQD Slots: Optimized for high-speed data transfer, ensuring the camera buffer never slows you down during intense bursts.
  • Built-in Connectivity: Includes 1000BASE-T Wired LAN (Ethernet), Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth for professional-grade file transmission to FTP servers.
  • Unstoppable Battery Life: Rated for approximately 3,580 shots per charge (using the EN-EL18c battery), making it ideal for long event days.
  • Group Area AF: Offers 17 custom Group Area AF patterns, allowing photographers to tailor the focus area to the specific movement of their subject.
