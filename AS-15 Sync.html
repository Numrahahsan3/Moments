Product Name:

AS-15 Sync Terminal

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon AS-15 is a highly useful and compact adapter designed for Nikon cameras that do not have a built-in PC sync terminal. This adapter fits onto the camera's standard ISO-type hot shoe and provides a sync terminal, allowing you to synchronize wired studio flashes or external lights with the camera. It is an essential accessory for photographers who want to use older studio lights or wired trigger systems.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Compatible with all Nikon SLR/DSLR and Mirrorless cameras with a standard ISO hot shoe.
  • Connector Type: Standard PC Sync Terminal (Female).
  • Mounting: Slides directly into the camera's Hot Shoe.
  • Circuitry: Simple pass-through (does not provide voltage protection).
  • Weight: Extremely lightweight, approx. 18g.
  • Dimensions: Compact design, approx. 25 x 28 x 22 mm.

Key Features:

  • Studio Connectivity: This adapter allows you to connect studio lights to cameras that are missing a sync port.
  • Secure Fit: Its design fits firmly onto the camera's hot shoe to ensure the connection remains stable during firing.
  • Compact & Portable: It is small enough that you can keep it in any pocket of your camera bag at all times.
  • Plug-and-Play: It requires no batteries or complex settings; simply attach it to the shoe and connect the wire.
  • Reliable Triggering: Due to the wired connection, there is no risk of wireless interference or signal drops.
  • Standard Design: It works with both old and new standard PC sync cables.
