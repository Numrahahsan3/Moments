Product Name:

Canon DM-E1D

Price:

$569

Description:

The Canon DM-E1D is a compact, high-performance directional stereo microphone that brings audio recording into the digital era. It mounts directly onto the camera's Multi-Function Shoe, drawing power from the camera and transmitting a digital signal to minimize interference. It features three pick-up modes—Shotgun, 90°, and 120°—giving you the flexibility to record everything from focused interviews to wide ambient soundscapes. Its cableless design and integrated menu button make it an ideal choice for professional videographers and vloggers using the latest EOS R-series cameras


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Canon cameras with a Multi-Function Shoe
  • Sensitivity (Mic): Shotgun (Mono): -16 dBFS (94 dBSPL @ 1 kHz)
  • Sensitivity (Line): Not applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None. It does not have any physical input ports (like a 3.5mm jack).
  • Weight: Approximately 136g (Body only).
  • Dimensions:Approximately 41.3 x 87.4 x 108.6 mm

Key Features:

  • Multi-Function Shoe Powered: Unlike traditional microphones, the DM-E1D does not use a 3.5mm audio cable. It slides into the camera's Multi-Function Shoe and communicates directly via digital pins.
  • No Battery Required: It draws power directly from the camera body, so you never have to worry about the microphone battery dying during a shoot.
  • Noise Reduction: Since the signal is digital from the microphone to the camera, there is significantly less "hiss" or electrical interference compared to analog cables.
  • Wind Screen Included: It comes with a "deadcat" (furry windscreen) to help block out wind noise when recording outdoors .
  • In-Camera Control: You can adjust the microphone settings (like gain and wind filter) directly through the camera’s touchscreen menu.
