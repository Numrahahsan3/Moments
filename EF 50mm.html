Product Name:

EF 50mm f/1.8 STM

Price:

$329

Description:

The EF 50mm f/1.8 STM is one of the most popular and essential prime lenses for Canon users, widely known as the "Nifty Fifty" due to its versatility and affordability. It features a wide maximum aperture of f/1.8, which allows for excellent low-light performance and creates a beautiful background blur (bokeh) that makes subjects stand out, making it ideal for portrait photography. The lens is equipped with a Stepping Motor (STM), providing near-silent and smooth continuous autofocus, which is particularly beneficial for video recording


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Fixed Focal Length)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 (Great for background blur/Bokeh)
  • Motor: STM (Stepping Motor) for smooth and silent autofocus
  • Construction: 6 Elements in 5 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.35m (1.15 ft)
  • Filter Size: 49mm

Key Features:

  • Full-Time Manual Focus:You can manually adjust the focus ring at any time, even when the lens is set to AF (Autofocus) mode.
  • Metal Lens Mount:Unlike its predecessor (the f/1.8 II), this version has a metal mount for better durability and a more secure fit to the camera body.
  • Close Focusing:It can focus as close as 0.35m (1.15 ft), allowing you to get nearer to your subject than previous models.
  • Beautiful Bokeh:With 7 rounded aperture blades, it creates a smooth, blurred background that makes your subject "pop," which is perfect for portraits.
  • Fixed 50mm Focal Length:Known as a "normal" lens because it provides a perspective similar to the human eye. On a crop-sensor (APS-C) camera, it acts like an 80mm lens, which is the ideal focal length for portraits.
