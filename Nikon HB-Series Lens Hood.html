Product Name:

Nikon HB-Series Lens Hood

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon HB-Series consists of high-quality, bayonet-mount lens hoods specifically engineered for Nikkor lenses. These hoods are essential for any photographer looking to improve image quality by blocking stray light from entering the lens, which significantly reduces flare and ghosting. Designed for a secure and seamless fit, the HB-series hoods also offer an extra layer of physical protection for the front element of your lens against accidental impact and rain.


Specifications

  • Mount Type: Bayonet-mount for quick, secure attachment and easy removal
  • Compatibility: Varies by model (e.g., HB-47 for 50mm f/1.8G, HB-32 for 18-105mm VR)
  • Material: Made from high-grade, impact-resistant ABS plastic with a matte interior finish
  • Storage: Reversible design allows the hood to be mounted backwards for compact storage in camera bags
  • Weight: Extremely lightweight, ranging from approximately 20g to 60g depending on the specific model

Key Features:

  • Flare Reduction: Effectively deflects stray light to improve contrast and saturation in your photographs.
  • Physical Protection: Acts as a buffer to protect the front glass of your lens from scratches, fingerprints, and bumps.
  • Matte Inner Finish: Features a non-reflective interior surface to further prevent internal reflections from reaching the sensor.
  • Reverse Mounting: The smart bayonet design allows you to flip the hood and store it on the lens when not in use.
  • Perfect Alignment: Specifically shaped (petal or cylindrical) to match the focal length and angle of view, preventing vignetting.
