Product Name:

Sony UWP-D21 Wireless Lavalier Microphone System

Price:

$599

Description:

The Sony UWP-D21 is a professional-grade wireless lavalier microphone system designed for content creators, videographers, and journalists who demand pristine audio quality. This system offers reliable digital audio processing for superb sound reproduction and utilizes a true diversity reception method to minimize signal dropouts.

One of its standout features is the "NFC Sync" capability, which allows for quick and secure frequency pairing between the transmitter and receiver with a single touch. Its compact, all-metal construction ensures durability in rugged field environments. Whether you are filming a documentary or conducting a high-stakes interview, the UWP-D21 provides the wireless stability and high-fidelity audio required for professional broadcast standards.

Specifications:

  • Frequency Range: UC14: 470.125 MHz to 541.875 MHz (UHF-TV channels 14 to 25)
  • Oscillator Type: Crystal-controlled PLL Synthesizer
  • Signal-to-Noise Ratio: 96dB (Max deviation, A-weighted)
  • Audio Delay: Approx. 0.35 ms
  • Input Connector: 3.5mm diameter 3-pole locking mini jack
  • Battery Life: Approx. 6 hours with Sony's AA-size alkaline batteries
  • Weight: Approx. 149g (including batteries)

Key Features:

  • Digital Audio Processing: Delivers high-quality sound by improving transient response performance between the transmitter and receiver.
  • NFC SYNC Function: Allows quick and easy secure channel setting by simply pressing the Sync button and bringing the devices close together.
  • True Diversity Reception: Two separate antennas ensure a stable signal and prevent interference by automatically switching to the stronger signal.
  • Auto Gain Mode: Reduces the risk of distortion by automatically setting the microphone's input level based on the speaker's volume.
  • OLED Display: High-visibility OLED screen provides clear information on battery life, signal strength, and RF levels even in bright sunlight.
