Sony LCS-U21 Soft Carrying Case

Price:

$35

Description:

The Sony LCS-U21 is a versatile, medium-sized soft carrying case designed to provide stylish protection for your Handycam camcorder, Alpha Mirrorless camera, or Cyber-shot digital camera. Engineered for photographers and videographers on the move, this case offers a perfect balance of lightweight portability and rugged durability. The padded interior features an adjustable divider, allowing you to customize the space for your specific gear. Whether you are trekking through the city or traveling abroad, the LCS-U21 ensures your equipment remains shielded from dust, scratches, and minor impacts. It features a wide-opening top flap for quick access to your camera so you never miss a shot, along with multiple side pockets for organized storage of accessories like batteries, memory cards, and charging cables.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Handycam Camcorders, Alpha Mirrorless, and High-zoom Cyber-shot Cameras
  • Material: Durable Polyamide (Nylon) for weather and wear resistance
  • Internal Divider: Movable padded divider for custom layout
  • Closure Type: Quick-release buckle and zipper for double security
  • External Pockets: 2 x Side pockets, 1 x Front pocket for accessories
  • Weight: Approximately 315g (11.2 oz)
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 26.0 x 19.0 x 17.0 cm (Approx.)

Key Features:

  • Easy Access: Large top lid makes it easy to grab your camera quickly, ensuring you’re always ready to record.
  • Secure Storage: Features a dual-safety system with a sturdy buckle and a reliable zipper to keep your gear tucked away safely.
  • Customizable Interior: The adjustable internal partition helps you separate the camera from additional lenses or batteries.
  • Dedicated Accessory Pockets: Multiple side and front compartments provide ample space for small items like lens caps and filters.
  • Comfortable Carrying: Comes with a comfortable shoulder strap and a padded top handle for multiple carrying options.
