Professional Wireless Bluetooth Remote

Price: $45.00

Description:

Take control of your photography with this Wireless Bluetooth Camera Remote. Perfect for long exposures, self-portraits, and group shots, this remote allows you to trigger your camera's shutter from a distance without any camera shake. It features a compact design and reliable connectivity for both photo and video modes.


Specifications:

  • Connectivity: Bluetooth Low Energy / Infrared
  • Operating Range: Up to 10 meters (32 feet)
  • Battery Type: CR2032 Lithium Coin Battery
  • Functions: Shutter Release, AF Start, Video Start/Stop
  • Weight: 25g (Ultra Lightweight)
  • Compatibility: Sony, Canon, and Nikon Mirrorless Cameras

Key Features:

  • Zero Camera Shake: Essential for sharp macro and night-sky photography.
  • Video Control: Dedicated button to start and stop video recording remotely.
  • Instant Pairing: Quick setup with built-in Bluetooth technology.
  • Focus Support: Two-stage button for autofocus before capturing the shot.
  • Weather Resistant: Sealed buttons for protection against light dust and moisture.
