Product Name:

Canon DM-E100

Price:

$579

Description:

The Canon DM-E100 is a "plug-and-play" directional stereo microphone. It features an electret condenser design with a 120° unidirectional pick-up pattern, providing high-quality stereo sound while reducing background noise. It is incredibly portable and draws power directly from the camera via the 3.5mm jack, so you never have to worry about batteries


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Works with Canon cameras that have a 3.5mm Microphone
  • Sensitivity: -40 dB ±4 dB (1 kHz, 0 dB=1 V/Pa)
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: It has a built-in 3.5mm Stereo Mini Plug (it is not cableless)
  • Weight: Approximately 64g (Extremely lightweight)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 30.5 x 66.5 x 88.2 mm.

Key Features:

  • No Batteries Required:The microphone uses "Plug-in Power" directly from the camera's 3.5mm jack. You never have to worry about charging it or the battery running out mid-video.
  • Easy Setup:Since there are no buttons or complex settings on the mic itself, you just plug it in and start recording.
  • Improved Clarity:It features a 97dB Signal-to-Noise ratio, which helps in capturing clear audio with minimal background "hiss.
  • 120° Unidirectional Stereo:It captures sound in a wide 120-degree arc. This provides a natural, immersive "stereo" feel, making it much better than the built-in mono microphone found on most cameras.
  • Wind Screen Included:It comes with a furry windscreen (deadcat). This is essential for outdoor shooting as it helps block out the "buffeting" sound caused by wind.
