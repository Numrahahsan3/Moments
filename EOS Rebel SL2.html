Product Name:

Canon EOS Rebel SL2

Price:

$530

Description:

The Canon EOS Rebel SL2 (known as the EOS 200D in some regions) is a remarkably compact and lightweight DSLR designed for photographers who want a portable yet powerful camera. It features a 24.2 Megapixel APS-C CMOS sensor paired with the DIGIC 7 image processor, ensuring high-quality images and fast performance. One of its standout features is the 3.0-inch Vari-angle Touch Screen, which makes it incredibly easy to shoot from creative angles and is perfect for vlogging. Additionally, it includes Dual Pixel CMOS AF for smooth and rapid autofocus during video and live view shooting, making it a favorite for beginners and enthusiasts alike


Specifications

  • Sensor: 24.2 Megapixel APS-C CMOS Sensor
  • Processor:DIGIC 7 Image Processor
  • Video: Full HD 1080p Video Recording at 60 fps
  • Range: ISO 100–25600 (Expandable to 51200)
  • Display: 3.0-inch Vari-angle Touch Screen LCD
  • Storage: SD, SDHC, and SDXC (UHS-I) Card support

Key Features:

  • Vari-angle Touch Screen:The 3.0-inch LCD can flip out and rotate 360 degrees, which is perfect for selfies, vlogging, and shooting from creative high or low angles.
  • DIGIC 7 Image Processor:This processor helps the camera handle low light better (ISO up to 25,600) and enables faster continuous shooting.
  • Wireless Connectivity:Built-in Wi-Fi, NFC, and Bluetooth allow you to transfer photos to your phone instantly or use your smartphone as a remote control.
  • Dual Pixel CMOS AF:This is the SL2's "superpower." It provides incredibly fast and smooth autofocus when using the screen (Live View) or recording video, making it a favorite for vloggers.
  • Full HD 60p Video & Mic Input: It records smooth 1080p video at 60 frames per second and includes a 3.5mm external microphone jack, which is essential for high-quality audio..
