SD (Secure Digital) Cards

Description:

The Professional SDXC UHS-II Card is a high-performance storage solution designed for Nikon DSLR and Mirrorless cameras. Engineered for photographers who demand speed and reliability, this card allows you to capture and transfer high-resolution images and cinema-quality 4K video. It leverages advanced UHS-II technology to provide read speeds that drastically reduce your post-production time. Whether you are shooting a wedding or a fast-paced sporting event, this card delivers the stability required for professional-grade content creation.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Ideal for Nikon Z-series, D850, D780, and high-end Mirrorless/DSLRs
  • Speed Class: UHS Speed Class 3 (U3), Video Speed Class 60 (V60), and Class 10
  • Read Speed: Up to 280 MB/s for rapid file transfer and workflow efficiency
  • Write Speed: Up to 150 MB/s for seamless RAW burst shooting
  • Interface: UHS-II (Backward compatible with UHS-I devices)
  • Durability: Built to be temperature, water, shock, and X-ray proof

Key Features:

  • 4K & 8K Ready: High-speed data rating ensures smooth recording of high-bitrate 4K UHD video without frame drops.
  • Rapid Burst Shooting: Optimized for high-speed continuous shooting, allowing your camera's buffer to clear faster.
  • Workflow Optimization: Extreme read speeds allow you to move large batches of RAW files to your computer in seconds.
  • Professional Build Quality: Designed for heavy daily use in demanding environments and extreme weather conditions.
  • Reliable Storage: Provides ample space for thousands of high-resolution RAW images and hours of professional video.
  • Data Protection: Engineered with high-quality flash memory to ensure long-term data integrity and safety.
