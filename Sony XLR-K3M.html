Product Name:

Sony XLR-K3M Digital XLR Adaptor Kit

Price:

$598

Description:

The Sony XLR-K3M is a professional dual-channel digital XLR audio adapter and microphone kit designed for Alpha series cameras and camcorders. It features a digital audio interface via the Multi Interface (MI) Shoe, allowing for high-quality, low-noise audio recording by transferring the digital signal directly to the camera. This setup eliminates the need for extra cables or batteries, making it an ideal choice for mobile journalists, wedding videographers, and professional content creators. The kit includes the ECM-XM1 shotgun microphone, providing a complete out-of-the-box solution for capturing directional sound with extreme clarity while maintaining a compact and lightweight profile for handheld shooting.

Specifications:

  • Interface: Digital/Analog (Selectable) via Multi Interface (MI) Shoe
  • Inputs: 2 x XLR/TRS Combo Connectors, 1 x 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack
  • Frequency Response: 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz
  • Sampling Frequency: 48 kHz (Digital Interface)
  • Bit Depth: 24-bit (Digital Interface)
  • Weight: 187g (Adaptor only) / 400g (With mic and cables)
  • Dimensions: 11.7 x 11.5 x 11.0 cm (Approx.)

Key Features:

  • Digital Audio Interface: Delivers pristine sound quality by transmitting a digital signal directly to the camera through the MI Shoe, bypassing analog noise.
  • Three Input Options: Equipped with two XLR/TRS combo jacks and an additional 3.5mm stereo mini jack for versatile microphone setups.
  • Advanced Control Panel: Features dedicated dials and switches for gain control, low-cut filter, and input level (Line/Mic/Mic+48V) for each channel.
  • Cable-Free Connection: Powers directly from the camera through the MI Shoe, reducing cable clutter and the need for external battery packs.
  • Included Shotgun Microphone: Comes bundled with the ECM-XM1 professional shotgun mic and a high-quality wind screen for outdoor recording.
  • Vibration-Suppression Design: Built-in shock and vibration mount reduces low-frequency handling noise during active filming.
