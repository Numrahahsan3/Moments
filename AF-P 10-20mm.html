Product Name:

AF-P 10-20mm VR (DX)

Price:

$279

Description:

The AF-P DX NIKKOR 10-20mm f/4.5-5.6G VR is an ultra-wide-angle zoom lens designed specifically for Nikon's DX-format DSLRs. It is perfect for capturing sprawling landscapes, impressive architecture, and tight interior spaces that require a wider field of view. Despite its compact and lightweight design, it features Nikon’s Vibration Reduction (VR) image stabilization, which helps ensure sharp handheld photos and steady video footage even in challenging lighting conditions.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Ultra-Wide Angle Zoom (DX Format)
  • Aperture: Variable Maximum f/4.5-5.6
  • Motor: AF-P (Pulse Motor) for ultra-fast and near-silent autofocus
  • Construction: 14 Elements in 11 Groups (including 3 Aspherical elements)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.22m (0.8 ft)
  • Filter Size: 72mm

Key Features:

  • Vibration Reduction (VR): Built-in image stabilization provides up to 3.5 stops of shake correction, which is crucial for handheld shooting and smooth video.
  • Ultra-Wide Perspective: With a 10-20mm range (15-30mm equivalent on full-frame), it allows you to fit massive subjects into the frame from a short distance.
  • Pulse Motor (AF-P): Uses a stepping motor for fast, quiet, and precise autofocus, making it excellent for vlogging and video recording without motor noise.
  • Compact & Lightweight: Weighing only about 230g, it is highly portable and easy to carry on long hikes or travel trips.
  • Superior Image Quality: Three aspherical lens elements help minimize distortion and aberrations, ensuring clear images from edge to edge.
  • Close Focusing: A minimum focus distance of 0.22m allows you to get very close to your foreground subject while still capturing the vast background.
