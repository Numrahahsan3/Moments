Product Name:

Canon Stereo Microphone SM-V1

Price:

$720

Description:

The Canon Stereo Microphone SM-V1 is a specialized 5.1-channel surround sound microphone. Unlike standard stereo mics, it uses four internal microphone capsules to capture audio from all directions (front, back, left, and right), which the camera then processes into a surround sound file. It is ideal for capturing "atmosphere" and immersive theater-quality audio


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Exclusively for Canon camcorders equipped with a Mini Advanced Shoe
  • Sensitivity: Approximately -41 dB (Omnidirectional)
  • Auxiliary Input: None. It does not have an extra port to plug in another device.
  • Input Terminals: None. This microphone does not have any physical input ports
  • Weight: Approximately 48g (1.7 oz)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 46 x 57 x 55 mm (1.8 x 2.2 x 2.2 in).

Key Features:

  • Directional Capture:It uses multiple microphone capsules to capture sound from the front, sides, and rear, creating a "theater-like" experience when played back on a surround sound system.
  • Immersive Audio:Unlike standard microphones that only record in mono or simple stereo, the SM-V1 is capable of recording 5.1-channel surround sound.
  • Wind Screen Included:It typically comes with a "furry" windscreen (deadcat) to reduce the distracting "buffeting" sound caused by wind when shooting outdoors .
  • Cable-Free Design:It connects directly to the camera via the Canon Mini Advanced Shoe. This means you don't need to plug in a separate 3.5mm audio cable.
  • No Batteries Needed: The microphone draws its power directly from the camcorder, so there is no need to worry about charging it separately.
