Product Name:

Canon Macro Twin Lite MT-26EX-RT

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon Macro Twin Lite MT-26EX-RT is a professional-grade lighting solution specifically engineered for close-up and macro photography. It features two independently adjustable flash heads that can be swiveled around the lens or tilted to provide precise control over shadows and highlights, which is essential for shooting small subjects like insects or jewelry. With a powerful Guide Number of 26m and the addition of 2.4 GHz radio wireless functionality, it allows photographers to control multiple off-camera flashes, providing a highly flexible and creative lighting environment.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed specifically for Canon EF and EF-S Macro lenses
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Approximately 570g
  • Dimensions: Control Unit: 69.5 x 112.5 x 90.0 mm
    Flash Unit (including ring): 232.2 x 91.1 x 49.1 mm

Key Features:

  • Dual-Head Design: Two independently adjustable flash heads that can be rotated and angled for precise lighting control.
  • Radio Wireless Control: Built-in 2.4GHz radio transmission allows it to act as a master unit to control other flashes wirelessly.
  • High Output Power: Offers a powerful Guide Number of approx. 26m (85.3 ft) at ISO 100, perfect for deep macro detail.
  • White LED Assist Lamps: High-brightness LED focus lamps help in achieving sharp focus in low-light macro environments.
  • Customizable Ratios: Easily adjust the flash ratio between the two heads to create depth and shadows on small subjects.
  • Fast Recycle Time: Improved charging circuit provides silent and quick recycling times for continuous shooting.
