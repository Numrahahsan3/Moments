Product Name:

Tripod Collar Mount Ring

Price:

$279

Description:

The Tripod Collar Mount Ring is an essential accessory for photographers using long, heavy telephoto lenses. By shifting the mounting point from the camera body to the lens barrel, it provides a better balance point for the entire setup. This prevents "lens flop," reduces strain on the camera's lens mount, and allows for much smoother rotation between landscape and portrait orientations without removing the camera from the tripod.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Specific to lens models (e.g., 70-200mm, 100-400mm, 300mm f/4)
  • Mounting Thread: Standard 1/4"-20 and often 3/8"-16 tripod sockets
  • Material: High-strength Aviation Aluminum (CNC machined)
  • Inner Diameter: Precision-fitted to specific lens barrel dimensions
  • Weight: Approximately 150g - 250g (depending on size)
  • Finish: Satin/Matte anodized finish for scratch resistance

Key Features:

  • Improved Balance: Centers the weight of the lens over the tripod head, ensuring stability and preventing the tripod from tipping over.
  • Quick Rotation: Allows you to loosen the knob and rotate the lens 90 degrees to switch between horizontal and vertical shots instantly.
  • Mount Protection: Safeguards the camera's bayonet mount by bearing the weight of heavy glass that would otherwise cause mechanical stress.
  • Arca-Swiss Compatibility: Many modern rings feature an integrated Arca-style dovetail foot for direct mounting to quick-release clamps.
  • Secure Locking: Features a dedicated locking knob and often a safety release to ensure the lens doesn't slip during adjustment.
