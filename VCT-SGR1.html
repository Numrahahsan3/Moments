Product Name:

Sony VCT-SGR1 Shooting Grip

Price:

$98

Description:

The Sony VCT-SGR1 is a unique and versatile shooting grip designed specifically for the RX100 and RX0 series cameras. This compact accessory serves as a dual-purpose tool: it functions as a stable handheld grip for capturing smooth video and photos while on the move, and it can also be instantly converted into a sturdy mini-tripod for stationary shots or self-portraits. Whether you are a vlogger, a travel enthusiast, or a street photographer, this grip enhances your ergonomics and provides integrated controls for shutter release, video start/stop, and zoom functions directly at your fingertips. Its lightweight design ensures that it won't weigh you down during long days of shooting.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Sony Cyber-shot RX100 (II, III, IV, V, VI, VII) and RX0 series
  • Maximum Load: Up to 1kg (35.3 oz)
  • Tilting Angle: Up (70 degrees), Down (100 degrees)
  • Connectivity: Multi/Micro USB Terminal
  • Material: High-quality Polycarbonate and Metal
  • Weight: Approximately 93g (3.3 oz)
  • Folded Height: 108.3 mm (4.3 in)

Key Features:

  • Integrated Remote Controls: Features dedicated buttons for Photo Shutter, Movie Start/Stop, and Zoom, allowing one-handed operation without touching the camera.
  • 2-in-1 Design: Easily converts from a comfortable handheld grip into a reliable mini-tripod for tabletop use or long-exposure shots.
  • Flexible Tilting: The adjustable head allows you to tilt the camera up or down, making it perfect for low-angle shots or overhead recording.
  • No Batteries Required: The unit draws its minimal power directly from the camera via the Multi-USB cable connection.
  • Compact & Portable: Small enough to fit in a pocket or a small camera bag, making it the ideal companion for compact camera users.
