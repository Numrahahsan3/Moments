Product Name:

Sony WLA-NS7 Wireless Transmitter

Price:

$59.99

Description:

The Sony WLA-NS7 Wireless Transmitter is a specialized device designed to bridge the gap between your BRAVIA XR TV and select Sony headphones. By connecting this compact transmitter to your TV's optical and USB ports, it unlocks the revolutionary 360 Spatial Sound Personalizer technology. This creates an immersive, cinematic audio environment tailored specifically to your ear shape using the 360 Spatial Sound Personalizer app.

Perfect for late-night movie sessions or gaming, it ensures a lag-free, high-quality audio connection without disturbing others. Whether you are using the SRS-NS7 wearable neckband speaker or supported Sony headphones, this transmitter provides a seamless and personalized Dolby Atmos® experience that makes you feel like you are right in the middle of the action.

Specifications:

  • Interface: Optical Digital Audio Input, USB Type-A (for Power)
  • Bluetooth Version: Bluetooth® 5.0 (Low Latency)
  • Support Audio Format: SBC (Subband Codec)
  • Transmission Range: Approx. 30 meters (Line of sight)
  • Personalization: Compatible with Sony 360 Spatial Sound Personalizer app
  • Weight: Approximately 29g (Lightweight and Portable)
  • Dimensions: 5.8 x 5.8 x 1.8 cm

Key Features:

  • 360 Spatial Sound: Tailors the sound field to your ears for a unique, immersive cinematic experience when paired with BRAVIA XR TVs.
  • Dolby Atmos® Compatibility: Enhances movie watching with multi-dimensional surround sound capabilities.
  • Low Latency Connection: Minimizes audio delay, making it ideal for high-paced gaming and synchronized video playback.
  • Plug and Play: Simple setup via optical cable for audio and USB cable for power—no complex configuration required.
  • Wide Compatibility: Works perfectly with Sony SRS-NS7 neckband speakers and a wide range of Sony Bluetooth headphones.
