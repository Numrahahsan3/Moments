Product Name:

MA-400 Adapter

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon MA-400 is a compact, shoe-mountable microphone adapter designed to expand the audio capabilities of compatible Canon cameras. It allows users to mount an external shotgun microphone and record high-quality audio independently via two professional XLR channels It is particularly useful for "run-and-gun" filmmakers and broadcast journalists who need a streamlined setup without bulky external mixers. The unit is powered directly by the camera’s power supply, meaning no extra batteries are required


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Canon Cinema EOS C300 Mark II and XC15 4K Camcorders
  • Sensitivity (Mic): -60 dBu / 600 Ohms
  • Sensitivity (Line): +4 dBu / 10 kOhms
  • Auxiliary Input: 1 x 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack
  • Input Terminals: 2 x 3-pin XLR Female (Pin 1: Shield, Pin 2: Hot, Pin 3: Cold)
  • Weight: Approximately 340g (12 oz)
  • Dimensions: 12.8 x 11.7 x 11.5 cm (5.0 x 4.6 x 4.5 in)

Key Features:

  • Two 3-Pin XLR Terminals: It provides two independent XLR inputs, allowing you to connect professional microphones (like shotgun or lavalier mics) or analog line-in sources.
  • 48V Phantom Power: The adapter supports 48V phantom power, which is necessary for using high-quality condenser microphones that don't have their own battery.
  • Powered by Camera: The MA-400 does not require its own batteries. It draws power directly from the camera body via the unit cable (typically the UN-5 cable).
  • Manual Controls: It usually features a protective translucent cover that hides physical switches and dials for audio levels, preventing accidental adjustments while allowing you to monitor settings.
  • Shoe-Mountable: The unit slides directly into the camera’s accessory shoe and locks into place with a secure screw mechanism.
