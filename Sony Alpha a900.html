Product Name:

Sony Alpha a900

Price:

$899

Description:

The Sony Alpha a900 is a flagship full-frame DSLR designed for serious photographers who demand uncompromising image quality. Featuring a massive high-resolution sensor and a bright optical glass pentaprism viewfinder, it offers a pure photographic experience with exceptional detail and color depth, making it a legendary choice for studio and landscape photography.


Specifications:

  • Sensor: 24.6 MP Full-Frame Exmor CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: Dual BIONZ Image Processing Engines
  • AF Points: 9-Point AF System with 10 Assist Points
  • ISO Range: 200-3200 (Expandable to ISO 100-6400)
  • Display: 3.0-inch 921k-dot Xtra Fine LCD

Key Features:

  • SteadyShot INSIDE: The world's first full-frame sensor-shift image stabilization system, compatible with all Sony A-mount lenses.
  • Optical Viewfinder: Ultra-bright pentaprism viewfinder with 100% frame coverage for precise manual focusing and framing.
  • Rugged Build: Durable magnesium alloy body panels and a high-performance shutter unit rated for 100,000 cycles.
  • Intelligent Preview: Allows you to check white balance and exposure on the LCD before taking the actual shot.
  • Dual Card Slots: Supports both CompactFlash and Memory Stick Duo for flexible storage options during long shoots.
