Product Name:

Nikon Z8

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon Z8 is a high-performance full-frame mirrorless camera that packs the flagship capabilities of the Z9 into a compact, agile body. Designed for professional photographers and videographers alike, it features a stacked CMOS sensor that eliminates the need for a mechanical shutter. Whether you are shooting high-speed sports, weddings, or cinematic 8K video, the Z8 provides the ultimate balance of portability and power, making it the true successor to the legendary D850 in the mirrorless era.


Specifications:

  • Sensor Type: 45.7MP Full-Frame Stacked CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: EXPEED 7 Image Processing Engine
  • Video Resolution: 8K/60p, 4K/120p (Internal N-RAW & ProRes RAW)
  • ISO Range: 64 - 25,600 (Expandable to 32 - 102,400)
  • Burst Speed: Up to 20 fps (Raw), 30 fps (JPEG), and 120 fps (11MP JPEG)
  • Storage Slots: Dual Slots (1x CFexpress Type B / XQD, 1x SD UHS-II)
  • Weight: Approximately 910g (2 lb.) with battery and card

Key Features:

  • No Mechanical Shutter: Thanks to its incredibly fast sensor readout, the Z8 uses a purely electronic shutter, offering silent shooting and a maximum shutter speed of 1/32,000s without wear and tear.
  • Subject Detection with AI: The advanced AF system uses deep learning to automatically detect and track people, dogs, cats, birds, cars, motorcycles, bicycles, trains, and planes.
  • Real-Live Viewfinder: A blackout-free viewfinder that provides a continuous, real-time view of the action, even when shooting at the highest burst rates.
  • Internal 12-Bit RAW Video: Allows filmmakers to record high-quality 12-bit N-RAW and Apple ProRes RAW HQ internally, providing massive flexibility in post-production color grading.
  • 4-Axis Tilting Monitor: The 3.2-inch touchscreen tilts both horizontally and vertically, allowing for easy framing from high or low angles in both landscape and portrait orientations.
  • Professional Build: Fully weather-sealed body made from eco-friendly carbon fiber composite and magnesium alloy, ensuring durability in extreme environments.
