Product Name:

ME-W1 Wireless Microphone

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon ME-W1 is a versatile, high-performance wireless microphone system designed for creators who need clear audio from a distance. Operating on Bluetooth technology, it allows you to record crisp mono or stereo sound (when used with the ME-1) from up to 50 meters away. It is an ideal solution for interviews, presentations, and vlogging where the subject is moving or far from the camera. The system consists of both a transmitter and a receiver, both of which are water-resistant and designed for outdoor use.


Specifications:

  • Communication System: Bluetooth Specification Power Class 1
  • Range: Up to 50m (approx. 164 ft) line of sight
  • Frequency Response: 300Hz to 9,000Hz
  • Power Source: 1 x AAA Alkaline or Ni-MH battery (for both transmitter and receiver)
  • Battery Life: Approximately 3 hours of continuous use
  • Connectors: 3.5mm Stereo Mini Jack (Output on Receiver / Input on Transmitter)
  • Weight: Approximately 32g (Transmitter) / 32g (Receiver)

Key Features:

  • Long Range Wireless: Records clear audio from subjects up to 50 meters away, perfect for wide shots and outdoor filming.
  • Two-Way Communication: The system allows the photographer and the subject to communicate via headphones, making it easier to give directions while recording.
  • Weather Resistant: Built with a "water-resistant" design (equivalent to IPX1), making it reliable for light rain or humid conditions.
  • Universal Compatibility: Works with any camera (Nikon or other brands) that has a 3.5mm microphone jack.
  • Compact & Lightweight: Small enough to fit in a pocket, it doesn't add significant weight to your camera rig.
  • Stereo Capability: While it records mono natively, you can plug the Nikon ME-1 microphone into the transmitter to record high-quality stereo sound wirelessly.
