Description:

The Sony CFexpress Type A TOUGH Series Card is a revolutionary storage solution designed for high-performance Sony Alpha cameras. Engineered for compact size without sacrificing speed, this card allows you to capture high-bitrate 4K 120p video and ultra-fast RAW photo bursts. With its advanced heat-dissipating design and TOUGH durability, it provides the reliability needed for professional creators who push their gear to the limit in extreme environments.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Ideal for Sony A1, A7S III, A7 IV, FX3, and FX30
  • Interface: PCIe Gen3 bus for professional-grade throughput
  • Read Speed: Up to 800 MB/s for lightning-fast file offloading
  • Write Speed: Up to 700 MB/s for high-resolution burst shooting
  • Video Performance: VPG-400 rated for stable, professional video recording
  • Durability: IP57 rated (Water/Dust proof) and 10x more bend-resistant than standard cards
