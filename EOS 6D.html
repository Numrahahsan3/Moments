Product Name:

Canon EOS 6D

Price:

$930

Description:

The Canon EOS 6D is a legendary full-frame DSLR designed for photographers who want professional-grade image quality in a more portable and lightweight body. It features a 20.2 Megapixel Full-Frame CMOS Sensor, which is particularly famous for its incredible performance in low-light situations and its ability to produce beautiful "bokeh" or blurred backgrounds. As the first EOS camera to include built-in Wi-Fi and GPS, it revolutionized how photographers shared their work and tracked their shooting locations on the go.


Specifications

  • Sensor: 20.2 Megapixel Full-Frame CMOS Sensor
  • Processor: DIGIC 5+ Image Processor
  • Video: Full HD 1080p Video Recording at 30fps
  • Range: ISO 100–25600 (Expandable to 50–102400)
  • Display: 3.0-inch Clear View LCD
  • Storage: SD, SDHC, or SDXC (UHS-I) Card support

Key Features:

  • 20.2MP Full-Frame CMOS Sensor:It features a large full-frame sensor that provides excellent shallow depth-of-field and professional-grade image quality, especially in low light .
  • Built-in Wi-Fi and GPS: The 6D was the first EOS camera to feature built-in Wi-Fi and GPS, allowing for easy image sharing and automatic geotagging of your travels .
  • Superb Low-Light Performance: With a native ISO range of 100–25,600 (expandable to 102,400), it is famous for its "clean" images in dark environments.
  • 11-Point AF System: While it has fewer focus points than the 90D, its center point is an "extra-sensitive" cross-type point that can focus in extremely dim light (down to -3 EV).
  • Full HD Video Recording: It captures high-quality 1080p video at 30, 25, or 24 fps, with manual exposure control and a built-in microphone jack for better audio.
