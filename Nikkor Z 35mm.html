Product Name:

Nikkor Z 35mm

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikkor Z 35mm f/1.8 S is a high-performance S-Line prime lens designed for Nikon's Z-mount mirrorless cameras. Known for its classic wide-angle perspective, this lens is a favorite for street photography, environmental portraits, and cinematic video. With a bright f/1.8 maximum aperture, it delivers exceptional sharpness across the entire frame and beautiful, natural bokeh. Its advanced optical design effectively suppresses aberrations, ensuring high resolution even when shooting wide open.


Specifications

  • Lens Type: Prime Lens (Z-Mount)
  • Aperture: Maximum f/1.8 (Excellent for low light and shallow depth of field)
  • Motor: Multi-focusing system for fast and accurate AF
  • Construction: 11 Elements in 9 Groups (includes 2 ED and 3 Aspherical elements)
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.25m (0.82 ft)
  • Filter Size: 62mm

Key Features:

  • S-Line Quality: Part of Nikon’s premium S-Line, ensuring the highest standards of optical performance, resolution, and build quality.
  • Nano Crystal Coat: Features anti-reflection coating that virtually eliminates internal lens element reflections, flares, and ghosting.
  • Weather Sealing: The lens barrel is extensively sealed to protect against dust and moisture, making it reliable in challenging weather conditions.
  • Customizable Control Ring: The ring can be used for manual focusing, aperture control, or exposure compensation for a smooth shooting experience.
  • Quiet Performance: The stepping motor (STM) provides ultra-quiet autofocus, which is crucial for capturing video without picking up motor noise.
  • Versatile 35mm Perspective: Offers a "human eye" view that is wide enough for landscapes but tight enough for portraits, making it the perfect "all-purpose" lens.
