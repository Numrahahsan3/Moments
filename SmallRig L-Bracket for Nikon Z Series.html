Product Name:

SmallRig L-Bracket for Nikon Z Series

Price:

$279

Description:

The SmallRig L-Bracket for Nikon Z Series (Z5/Z6/Z7/Z6II/Z7II) is a precision-engineered accessory designed to enhance your camera's versatility and handling. Its primary function is to allow seamless switching between horizontal (landscape) and vertical (portrait) orientations without changing the optical axis. This bracket is an essential tool for landscape, architectural, and studio photographers who require stability and speed. It features a form-fitting design that maintains full access to the camera's battery door and side ports, while adding a comfortable grip extension for better ergonomics.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Nikon Z5, Z6, Z7, Z6II, and Z7II mirrorless cameras
  • Mounting Type: Arca-Swiss style dovetails on both axes
  • Material: Aircraft-grade Aluminum Alloy (CNC machined)
  • Side Plate: Extendable (up to 20mm) for tethering or cable clearance
  • Mounting Thread: Multiple 1/4"-20 accessory threads
  • Weight: Approximately 140g - 155g (depending on specific model)

Key Features:

  • Quick Switch System: Arca-type compatible base and side plates allow for rapid mounting and dismounting on tripod heads in both orientations.
  • Extendable Side Plate: The side part can be pulled out to create space for HDMI, USB, or microphone cables when shooting vertically.
  • Perfect Integration: Custom-tailored to the Z series body, ensuring it doesn't block the tilting screen, battery compartment, or buttons.
  • Anti-Twist Design: Equipped with a safety screw and rubber cushions on the base plate to prevent the camera from twisting or scratching.
  • Built-in Tool: Features a magnetically stored flat-head screwdriver on the bottom for quick tightening or adjustments on the go.
