Product Name:

Sony VCT-MP1 Multipod

Price:

$198

Description:

The Sony VCT-MP1 Multipod is a versatile 4-way photography support system designed for creators who need flexibility without carrying multiple heavy accessories. This unique kit functions as a high-quality monopod, a mini tripod (tabletop stand), and a full-size support when combined. It is engineered specifically for Handycam camcorders and Alpha interchangeable lens cameras, providing incredible stability for both video recording and still photography. Whether you are shooting in a crowded event where space is limited or capturing low-angle macro shots on a desk, the VCT-MP1 adapts to your environment instantly. Its lightweight yet durable construction makes it an essential companion for travel vloggers and professional photographers who demand professional results in a compact form factor.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Most Sony Alpha, Cyber-shot, and Handycam cameras
  • Maximum Height: Approx. 1,530 mm (60.2 inches)
  • Minimum Height: Approx. 585 mm (23 inches)
  • Weight Capacity: Supports equipment up to 2.0 kg (4.4 lbs)
  • Material: High-grade Aluminum and Reinforced Composite
  • Weight: Approximately 935g (Including Mini Tripod)
  • Folded Length: 58.5 cm for easy portability

Key Features:

  • 4-Way Versatility: Can be used as a monopod, a monopod with a stabilizer stand, a mini tripod for tabletop use, or a standard tripod height support.
  • Quick Release System: Features a reliable quick-shoe mechanism that allows you to attach or detach your camera in seconds.
  • Compact & Travel-Ready: The modular design allows it to break down into smaller components, making it easy to fit into standard camera bags.
  • Adjustable Height: Multi-section legs with secure flip-locks allow for rapid height adjustments even on uneven terrain.
  • Independent Mini Tripod: The base can be detached and used as a standalone mini tripod, perfect for low-angle shots or webcams.
