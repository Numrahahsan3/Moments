Description:

The Professional CFexpress Type B Card is a next-generation storage solution designed for high-end mirrorless cameras and professional cinema rigs. Engineered for elite photographers and cinematographers, this card allows you to quickly capture and transfer ultra-high-quality RAW photos and breathtaking 8K video. With its PCIe Gen 3 and NVMe protocol support, it delivers industry-leading speeds that dramatically accelerate your professional workflow from capture to post-production. Whether you are shooting high-speed sports or cinematic masterpieces, this card provides the extreme performance and rugged durability required for the most demanding assignments.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Ideal for Nikon Z8, Z9, Z6II/Z7II, and professional Cinema Cameras
  • Interface: PCIe Gen3x2 with NVMe protocol for maximum throughput
  • Read Speed: Up to 1750 MB/s for instantaneous file offloading
  • Write Speed: Up to 1500 MB/s for uninterrupted high-bitrate recording
  • Video Performance: VPG-400 rated (or equivalent) for sustained video write speeds
  • Durability: Built to be shock, vibration, and extreme temperature resistant

Key Features:

  • 8K RAW Capability: Blazing-fast write speeds allow for flawless recording of 8K RAW and 4K 120fps video without any buffering.
  • Next-Gen Performance: Utilizes NVMe technology to outperform traditional SD and XQD cards by a significant margin.
  • Instant Workflow: The massive 1750MB/s read speed enables you to transfer terabytes of footage to your workstation in minutes.
  • Professional Reliability: Rigorously tested for sustained performance, making it the standard for high-end commercial and wildlife photography.
  • Rugged Build: Designed with a reinforced casing to withstand the rigors of professional use in the field and extreme environments.
  • High-Capacity Storage: Available in large capacities to handle the massive file sizes of high-resolution RAW burst shooting.
