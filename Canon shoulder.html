Product Name:

Canon Shoulder Support Unit SU-15

Price:

$270

Description:

The Canon Shoulder Support Unit SU-15 is a professional-grade accessory designed to transform high-end cinema cameras into an ENG-style (Electronic News Gathering) handheld configuration. It features an adjustable shoulder pad that allows operators to find the perfect center of gravity, even when using heavy lenses or large battery packs. Additionally, it includes integrated rod supports that allow for the mounting of essential cinema accessories like matte boxes, follow focus systems, and the SG-1 Shoulder-Style Grip.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed specifically for high-end Canon Cinema EOS cameras, including the EOS C700, C500 Mark II, C300 Mark III, and C200
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Approximately 1.34 kg (2.95 lbs) for the full unit.
  • Dimensions: Approximately 360.7 x 233.7 x 91.4 mm ($14.2 \times 9.2 \times 3.6$ inches).

Key Features:

  • Ergonomic Shoulder Pad: Features a soft, adjustable shoulder pad that provides comfort during long handheld shooting sessions.
  • Standard 15mm Rod Support: Includes dual 15mm rod clamps, making it compatible with industry-standard follow focus, matte boxes, and lens supports.
  • Toolless Adjustment: Designed for quick adjustments in the field, allowing you to balance the camera rig without needing extra tools.
  • Enhanced Stability: Distributes the weight of heavy cinema camera setups across the shoulder to reduce arm fatigue and camera shake.
  • Professional Rig Integration: Specifically engineered to work seamlessly with Canon Cinema EOS camera baseplates and accessories.
