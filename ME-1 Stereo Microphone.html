Product Name:

Nikon ME-1 Stereo Microphone

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon ME-1 is a dedicated external stereo microphone designed to expand the video recording capabilities of compatible Nikon DSLR and COOLPIX cameras. It features a unidirectional, stereo design that captures clear, high-quality sound while significantly reducing autofocus noise and other mechanical vibrations. Compact and lightweight, it is an essential tool for videographers who want to achieve professional-grade audio without the bulk of larger recording setups.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Nikon DSLRs, Mirrorless, and COOLPIX cameras with a 3.5mm Mic Jack
  • Type: Back-electret condenser microphone
  • Frequency Response: 70 Hz to 16,000 Hz
  • Sensitivity: -42 dB (0 dB = 1V/Pa, 1 kHz)
  • Signal-to-Noise Ratio: 60 dB or more
  • Weight: Approximately 80g (2.8 oz) — Microphone only
  • Connector: 3.5 mm gold-plated L-shaped stereo mini-pin jack

Key Features:

  • Stereo Sound: Engineered specifically for DSLR video, it records high-fidelity stereo sound that is far superior to the camera’s built-in mono microphone.
  • Vibration Reduction: It features an integrated vibration isolation system (vibration damper) that minimizes unwanted mechanical noise from the camera's autofocus and handling.
  • Low-Cut Filter: The microphone includes a user-selectable low-cut filter to reduce wind noise and other low-frequency interference, ensuring clearer outdoor recordings.
  • Powered by Camera: It uses "Plug-in Power," meaning it draws energy directly from the camera's microphone terminal, so there is no need for separate batteries.
  • Water-Resistant Design: Built with a durable body and a foam windscreen, it is designed to perform reliably in various shooting environments.
  • Hot-Shoe Mount: The ME-1 easily slides into the camera’s standard hot shoe and features a locking ring to keep it securely in place during movement.
