Product Name:

Macro Ring Lite MR-14EX II

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon Macro Ring Lite MR-14EX II is a high-performance electronic flash specifically engineered for close-up and macro photography. It features a unique twin-tube design where both flash tubes can be fired together for even, shadowless lighting, or independently to create a sense of depth and texture. With advanced features like E-TTL II autoflash and high-intensity white LED focusing lamps, it provides professional photographers and hobbyists with precise control over their lighting in challenging close-up environments.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed specifically for Canon EF Macro lenses (like the EF 100mm f/2.8L Macro IS USM).
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Approximately 455g
  • Dimensions: Control Unit: Approximately 69.6 x 118.8 x 71.4 mm
    Flash Unit (Ring): Approximately 129.6 x 112.1 x 25.3 mm

Key Features:

  • Twin-Tube Design: Do flash tubes hain jinhein aap alag-alag control kar sakte hain taake lighting mein depth aur shadow create ki ja sake.
  • High Power Output: Iska Guide Number 14m (ISO 100 par) hai, jo macro shots ke liye zabardast brightness deta hai.
  • E-TTL II Autoflash: Ye Canon ke E-TTL II system ko support karti hai, jis se accurate flash exposure automatically set ho jata hai.
  • LED Focus Lamps: Is mein do safaid LED lamps lage hain jo low light mein focus karne mein madad dete hain.
  • Wireless Master Flash: Ye doosri Speedlite flashes ko wireless control karne ki salahiyat rakhti hai.
  • Fast Recycle Time: Iska charging time bohot kam hai, taake aap baghair ruke shots le sakein.
