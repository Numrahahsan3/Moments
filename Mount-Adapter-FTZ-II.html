Product Name:

Nikon Mount Adapter FTZ II

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon Mount Adapter FTZ II is an advanced accessory that acts as a robust bridge between Nikon Z series mirrorless cameras and F-mount NIKKOR lenses. It is designed to maintain the exceptional quality of legacy lenses on the new mirrorless system. Compared to the previous model, the FTZ II features a more streamlined and compact design, providing extra clearance for fingers when using vertical grips and various accessories. It is the perfect tool for photographers who want to utilize their existing F-mount lens collection without any loss in image quality.


Specifications

  • Lens Compatibility: Compatible with 360+ NIKKOR F-mount lenses (from AI types to modern AF-S/AF-P)
  • Camera Compatibility: All Nikon Z series (Full-frame and APS-C) mirrorless cameras
  • Mounting: Front: Nikon F-mount; Back: Nikon Z-mount
  • Construction: Weather-sealed (dust and drip resistant) magnesium alloy chassis
  • Weight: Approximately 125g (Lightweight and portable)
  • Dimensions: Approximately 70 mm x 70 mm (Slim design without tripod socket)

Key Features:

  • Seamless Integration: Maintains identical image quality, autofocus speed, and metering performance when using F-mount lenses on Z series cameras.
  • Compact Design: The bulky tripod mount has been removed, making it more comfortable to use with large cameras like the Nikon Z9 and vertical grips.
  • Full AF/AE Support: Full autofocus and auto-exposure are supported when used with AF-S, AF-I, and AF-P lenses.
  • VR Enhancement: If your lens has Vibration Reduction (VR), it works in tandem with the camera's internal VR. If the lens lacks VR, the camera's In-Body Image Stabilization (IBIS) can still be utilized.
  • Weather Sealing: Features rubber seals to protect against dust and moisture, matching the durability of Nikon’s professional lens lineup.
