Product Name:

Canon BR-E1 Wireless Remote Control

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon BR-E1 is a slim, handheld Bluetooth remote designed for photographers and vloggers who need to operate their camera from a distance. Unlike traditional infrared remotes, it does not require a direct line of sight to the camera, meaning you can trigger the shutter even if the remote is in your pocket or behind the camera. It is perfect for group portraits, long exposures, and self-recording, offering full control over the shutter, video start/stop, and autofocus.


Specifications

  • Connectivity: Uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for a reliable, battery-efficient connection without needing a line of sight.
  • Operating Range : Works from a distance of approximately 5 meters (16.4 feet) in any direction.
  • Multi-Function Controls: Features dedicated buttons for Shutter Release, AF Start, and Video Recording, as well as a zoom button for compatible Power Zoom lenses.
  • Battery Life: Powered by a single CR2032 lithium button battery, which typically lasts for up to one year of regular use.
  • Compact Design: Extremely portable, weighing only 14g and measuring approximately 27 x 91 x 12 mm.
