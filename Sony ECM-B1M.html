Product Name:

Sony ECM-B1M Shotgun Microphone

Price:

$349

Description:

The Sony ECM-B1M is a high-performance, ultra-compact shotgun microphone designed specifically for Sony’s Alpha series cameras equipped with a Multi Interface (MI) Shoe. Utilizing advanced digital signal processing (DSP) and eight high-performance microphone capsules, this unit offers three selectable pickup patterns in one single mic: super-directional, unidirectional, and omnidirectional. What sets it apart is its completely cable-free and battery-free operation; it draws power and transmits digital audio directly through the camera's shoe. This makes it an ideal solution for content creators, vloggers, and professional videographers who demand studio-quality sound with a minimal footprint and maximum portability.


Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Sony Alpha cameras with Multi Interface (MI) Shoe (Digital/Analog support)
  • Microphone Type: Back electret condenser (Monaural)
  • Pickup Patterns: Super-directional / Unidirectional / Omnidirectional (Selectable)
  • Frequency Response: 40 Hz to 20,000 Hz
  • Audio Transmission: Digital (via MI Shoe) or Analog (switchable)
  • Weight: Approx. 77.3g (2.8 oz) - Ultra lightweight
  • Dimensions: 27.4 x 81.8 x 99.3 mm (1-1/8 x 3-1/4 x 4 in)

Key Features:

  • Three Pickup Patterns: Easily switch between Super-directional (focuses on front), Unidirectional (wider front), and Omnidirectional (all around) using a physical dial on the back.
  • Digital Audio Interface: When used with compatible cameras, the digital signal processing ensures crystal clear sound without the noise associated with analog cables.
  • Compact and Cable-Free: No XLR cables or 3.5mm jacks are required. It slides into the MI Shoe, keeping your setup clean and professional.
  • Advanced Noise Suppression: Features a low-cut filter and digital noise-cut filter to eliminate wind noise and low-frequency hum, ensuring voice clarity.
  • Battery-Free Operation: The microphone is powered directly by the camera body, so you never have to worry about charging it or running out of juice mid-shoot.
