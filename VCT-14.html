Product Name:

VCT-14 Quick Release Tripod Adapter

Price:

$295

Description:

The VCT-14 Quick Release Tripod Adapter is an industry-standard mounting solution designed for professional broadcast and cinema cameras. It features a heavy-duty metal construction that ensures maximum stability and durability in high-pressure filming environments. This adapter allows camera operators to quickly switch between handheld shooting and tripod mounting without the need for tools. With its precision-engineered locking mechanism, it provides a "rock-solid" connection that eliminates any wobble or movement, making it essential for documentary, news gathering (ENG), and commercial production workflows.

Specifications:

  • Compatibility: Standard Broadcast Shoulder Cameras and Cine Rigs with V-wedge plates
  • Material: High-Grade Aluminum Alloy and Stainless Steel
  • Mounting Interface: V-lock Quick Release System
  • Base Mounting: Multiple 1/4"-20 and 3/8"-16 Tripod Threads
  • Locking Mechanism: Spring-loaded Safety Release Lever
  • Weight: Approximately 850g (1.87 lbs)
  • Dimensions: 28.2 x 9.5 x 2.7 cm (11.1 x 3.7 x 1.0 in)

Key Features:

  • Instant Quick Release: Features a high-precision V-wedge design that allows for instantaneous mounting and unmounting of professional cameras.
  • Heavy-Duty Stability: Built with premium materials to support heavy camera rigs, including full-sized ENG camcorders with large lenses and batteries.
  • Safety Lock System: Integrated safety release lever prevents accidental camera detachment, ensuring your expensive equipment stays secure.
  • Universal Tripod Compatibility: Equipped with standard 1/4" and 3/8" threaded holes on the bottom, making it compatible with almost any professional tripod head.
  • Tool-less Operation: Designed for speed and efficiency, the locking and unlocking process requires no screwdrivers or Allen keys on set.
