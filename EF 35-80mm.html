Product Name:

EF 35-80mm F4-5.6 III

Price:

$644

Description:

The EF 35-80mm f/4-5.6 III is a compact and lightweight standard zoom lens originally designed as a versatile "kit lens" for Canon’s film and digital SLR cameras. It covers a practical focal range, from a wide-angle 35mm—perfect for landscapes and group shots—to a short 80mm telephoto, which is suitable for portraits. This third iteration of the lens features a simplified optical design to keep it portable and affordable, making it a popular choice for beginners who want a single lens to handle various everyday shooting situations.


Specifications

  • Lens Type:Standard Zoom Lens
  • Aperture:f/4 – f/5.6
  • Motor: DC Motor (Micro-motor)
  • Construction: 6 Elements in 6 Groups
  • Minimum Focus Distance:0.40m (1.3 ft)
  • Filter Size:52mm

Key Features:

  • Versatile Focal Length: The 35mm to 80mm range covers everything from moderately wide-angle (landscapes, group shots) to short telephoto (portraits).
  • Full-Frame Compatible: It is designed for full-frame cameras, but if used on a Crop Sensor (APS-C) camera, it behaves like a 56-128mm lens, which is excellent for portraits.
  • Close Focusing: It has a minimum focusing distance of 0.4m (1.3 ft). At the 80mm setting, it can fill the frame with an object the size of a postcard, making it decent for basic close-up photography.
  • DC Micromotor: It uses a standard micromotor for autofocus. While reliable, it is louder and slower compared to modern STM or USM motors.
  • Aspherical Lens Element: It includes one aspherical element to help reduce distortion and improve image contrast.
