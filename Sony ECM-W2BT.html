Product Name:

Sony ECM-W2BT Wireless Microphone

Price:

$229

Description:

The Sony ECM-W2BT is a state-of-the-art wireless microphone system designed for creators, vloggers, and journalists who demand high-quality audio without the hassle of cables. It features a compact transmitter with an internal microphone and a receiver that attaches directly to Sony cameras via the Multi Interface (MI) Shoe. This setup allows for a completely cable-free digital audio interface, significantly reducing noise and interference. Whether you are recording an interview, a product review, or a travel vlog, the ECM-W2BT provides reliable, clear sound even at distances up to 200 meters. Its dual-channel recording capability allows for simultaneous audio capture from both the subject and the camera operator, making it an incredibly versatile tool for any production environment.

Specifications:

  • Wireless Type: Bluetooth 5.0 (Qualcomm aptX Low Latency)
  • Maximum Range: Up to 200m (650 ft) in unobstructed areas
  • Audio Interface: Digital (via MI Shoe) or 3.5mm Analog Output
  • Microphone Polar Pattern: Omnidirectional (built-in)
  • Battery Life: Up to 9 hours (with MI Shoe connection)
  • Weather Resistance: Dust and Moisture Resistant Design
  • Weight: Transmitter: 27g | Receiver: 28g

Key Features:

  • Digital Audio Interface: When used with a Sony camera equipped with an MI Shoe that supports digital audio, the sound is transmitted directly to the camera without signal degradation.
  • Simultaneous Recording: The receiver features its own built-in microphone, allowing the camera operator to record their own voice alongside the subject's voice.
  • Three Pickup Modes: Choose between "MIC" (subject only), "MIX" (subject and operator), or "RCVR" (operator only) to suit your specific shooting scenario.
  • External Mic Input: The transmitter includes a 3.5mm stereo mini-jack, allowing you to plug in a lavalier microphone for a more discreet setup.
  • Stable Connectivity: Uses Bluetooth with aptX Low Latency to ensure clear audio sync and a stable connection even in crowded wireless environments.
