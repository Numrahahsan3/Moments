Product Name:

Tripod Grip HG-100TBR

Price:

$279

Description:

The Canon Tripod Grip HG-100TBR is a versatile 2-in-1 accessory designed primarily for vloggers and content creators. It functions as both a comfortable handheld shooting grip for stable video and a sturdy tabletop mini-tripod for stationary shots. The grip features a tilting and rotating head that allows for creative angles, including vertical video for social media. A standout feature is the included detachable BR-E1 Wireless Remote Control, which fits directly into the handle, allowing you to trigger the shutter, start/stop recording, and even operate zoom from a distance.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Designed for select Canon mirrorless and compact cameras, including the EOS R series
  • Sensitivity: Not Applicable
  • Auxiliary Input: None
  • Input Terminals: None
  • Weight: Grip only: Approximately 163g
  • Dimensions: As a Grip: Approx. 38.6 x 190.0 x 43.4 mm
    As a Tripod: Approx. 172.4 x 148.5 x 174.1 mm

Key Features:

  • 2-in-1 Design: Works as a handheld shooting grip and converts easily into a stable tabletop tripod.
  • Wireless Control: Includes a detachable BR-E1 Bluetooth remote for shutter, record, and zoom functions.
  • Flexible Angles: Features a tilting and rotating head for creative overhead shots or vertical social media video.
  • Lightweight & Compact: Weighing only 163g, it is highly portable and perfect for vloggers on the go.
  • Microphone Mount: Includes a dedicated microphone attachment bracket for better audio setups.
