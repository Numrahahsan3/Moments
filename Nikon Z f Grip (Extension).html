Product Name:

Nikon Z f Grip (Extension)

Price:

$279

Description:

The Nikon Z f-GR1 Extension Grip is a dedicated accessory designed specifically for the Nikon Z f mirrorless camera. While the Z f boasts a stunning retro aesthetic, its flat front can sometimes be challenging to hold during long shoots. This extension grip provides a more substantial handhold, significantly improving ergonomics and handling without sacrificing the camera's classic look. It features a built-in Arca-Swiss compatible track, making it a seamless addition for photographers who frequently switch between handheld and tripod shooting.


Specifications

  • Compatibility: Exclusively designed for the Nikon Z f mirrorless camera
  • Mounting Interface: Secure 1/4"-20 screw with locating pin
  • Tripod Compatibility: Integrated Arca-Swiss compatible dovetail groove
  • Construction: Premium synthetic leather finish over a sturdy frame
  • Battery Access: Custom cutout for uninterrupted battery and SD card access
  • Weight: Approximately 100g (Lightweight yet durable)

Key Features:

  • Enhanced Ergonomics: Adds a pronounced finger grip to the front of the camera, providing a more secure and comfortable hold for all hand sizes.
  • Arca-Swiss Ready: The base of the grip functions as a quick-release plate, allowing you to slide the camera directly onto Arca-style tripod heads.
  • Seamless Design: Wrapped in the same high-quality synthetic leather as the camera body to maintain the Z f's iconic vintage aesthetic.
  • Full Accessibility: Precision-engineered so you don't have to remove the grip to swap batteries or access the memory card slot.
  • Tool-less Mounting: Features a convenient D-ring screw on the bottom, allowing for quick installation or removal using just your fingers.
